Chicken Fajitas

Chicken thighs marinated with lime, soy, spices, cilantro, and garlic are seared and served with peppers, onion, and warm tortillas.

Yield: 6 • Prep: 25 min • Cook: 20 min • Total: 45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 pounds chicken thighs (you can sub chicken breast*)
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice (from 1 lime)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon liquid smoke
  • 2 tablespoons cumin
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt (use a little less if you have table salt)
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper ((or more, if you like it spicy!))
  • 1/3 cup chopped cilantro ((plus more to garnish, optional) )
  • 4 cloves garlic (smashed and minced)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (for searing chicken)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (for searing chicken)
  • 3 small red, yellow, or orange bell peppers (sliced)
  • 1 medium onion (sliced)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt (for onions and peppers)
  • 12 6-inch flour tortillas (sometimes labeled "fajita size")
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese** (optional, technically. See note for more ideas!)
  • sour cream
  • Chipotle Mayo
  • Guacamole
  • lime wedges
  • cilantro (chopped)

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken thighs against the grain into strips about 1 inch wide and place them in a bowl.
  2. Mix in the lime juice, soy sauce, liquid smoke, cumin, 2 teaspoons kosher salt, chili powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, cayenne, cilantro, and garlic. Coat the chicken evenly and marinate for 15 minutes, or refrigerate for up to 24 hours.
  3. Cut the bell peppers into 1/4-inch strips and slice the onion into 1/4-inch pieces.
  4. Heat a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for at least 2 minutes. Add 2 tablespoons vegetable oil and swirl to coat.
  5. Let excess marinade drip from the chicken before searing it in uncrowded batches, leaving 1-2 inches between strips. Take care around splattering oil. Cook the first side for about 2 minutes, until blackened.
  6. Add 1 tablespoon butter, turn the chicken, and cook the second side for 1-2 minutes. The largest piece should read 155 degrees on a thermometer as described by the source; carryover heat will continue cooking it. Alternatively, cut a piece open and check that it is white rather than pink.
  7. Repeat with the remaining chicken and reserve the marinade. Move the chicken to a plate and tent it with foil to keep warm.
  8. Add 2 more tablespoons cooking oil to the skillet. Add the peppers, onion, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and the reserved marinade; stir up the browned bits and saute for 2-3 minutes, until crisp-tender or cooked to your preference.
  9. Remove the skillet from the heat. Cut larger chicken pieces into bite-size pieces and return the chicken and any resting juices to the pan.
  10. Warm the tortillas one at a time in a clean skillet over medium heat. If using cheese, add it and heat until melted; fold the tortillas and keep them warm.
  11. Serve the chicken and vegetables in the warm tortillas with sour cream or chipotle mayo, lime, and chopped cilantro, as desired.

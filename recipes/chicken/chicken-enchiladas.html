Chicken Enchiladas

6 frozen chicken tenders

2 cans chicken broth

Chicken taco seasoning*

1 Tablespoon olive oil

1 (10 oz) bag of spinach

4 oz. baby portabella mushrooms

1 (4 oz) can diced green chilis

½ cup sour cream

12 oz bottle 505 green chili sauce

3 cups cheese

¼ cup cilantro

3 small avocados

6 tortillas

10 oz black beans** (can either be mixed in with the tortilla filling, or served on the side)

*Chicken Taco Seasoning

1 teaspoon chili powder

1 teaspoon coriander powder

1 teaspoon paprika

1/2 to 1 teaspoon salt, use as per your taste

1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

1/2 teaspoon onion powder

1/2 teaspoon cumin powder

1/2 teaspoon black pepper powder

1/2 teaspoon Mexican oregano

1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper powder

**Black Beans

Season with onion powder, garlic salt, cayenne pepper, a spoonful of salsa, and a couple of tablespoons of cojita cheese


Put frozen chicken tenders in chicken broth with ½ of the chicken taco seasoning. Reserve the rest for later. Boil chicken until easily shredded with a fork. Remove chicken from broth and shred.

While chicken is cooking, chop & cook mushrooms in olive oil, after they are mostly cooked, add spinach and cook until soft.

Mix shredded chicken, spinach, mushrooms, green chilis, sour cream, ½ of the 505 sauce, ½ of the cheese, and cilantro (and black beans if mixing in instead of serving on the side). Fill each tortilla with 1/6 of filling and top with avocado and place in 9x11 pan. Top pan of enchiladas with remainder of 505 sauce and cheese.

Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes. Let enchiladas rest for 10 minutes after they come out of the oven.

Provolone & Mushroom Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper and ¼ teaspoon ground red pepper
  • 4 6-oz skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 8 slices of toasted sourdough bread
  • 4 slices provolone cheese
  • ¼ cup trimmed arugula

Mushroom sauce

  • 2 8-oz pkgs mushrooms
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ¼ cup finely chopped shallots
  • 1 ½ teaspoons chopped fresh thyme
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ cup light sour cream

Instructions

  1. Place mushrooms in a food processor and process until finely chopped. Heat olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms, shallots, thyme, and garlic; cook for 10 minutes or until liquid evaporates. Stirring occasionally. Stir in salt and pepper.
  2. Place mushroom mixture in a bowl, stir in sour cream.
  3. (also great as a dip, serve at room temperature or chilled)
  4. Heat 2 teaspoons olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Rub salt, and peppers evenly over chicken. Place chicken in pan, cook 4 minutes on each side or until done. Spread mushroom sauce over each toasted bread slice, top with a chicken breast half, a slice of cheese and arugula and top with another slice of bread.

Chicken Caesar Wraps

chicken tenderloins (whatever amount you need for your family)
lemon juice
garlic salt

onion salt

cayenne pepper

seasoning salt

jalapeño cheddar wraps

Caesar dressing

muenster cheese

avocado


Cook chicken soaked in lemon juice and generously sprinkled with garlic salt, onion salt, and go a little easier on the cayenne pepper and seasoning salt. As you cook the chicken cut w/your spatula into bite size pieces.

Serve w/ jalapeño cheddar wraps, muenster cheese and chopped avocado; use Caesar dressing as sauce.

Hot Chicken Salad

Ingredients

  • 4 T. Flour
  • 4 T. butter
  • 14 oz. can seasoned (roasted garlic) Chicken Broth
  • 1 C. Diced Celery
  • 2 tsp. Minced Green Onion (use a little stem)
  • ¾ C. Mayo
  • 50 oz. canned white chicken meat
  • ½ C. Almonds
  • 2 Hard Boiled Eggs (chopped)
  • ½ tsp. Salt
  • ¼ tsp. Pepper
  • ¼ tsp. Worcestershire Sauce
  • 1 T. Lemon Juice
  • 1 C. Crushed potato Chips

Instructions

  1. Melt margarine, add flour and chicken broth. Wisk until creamy.
  2. Mix sauce with other ingredients. Sprinkle top with chips and bake uncovered in casserole dish at 400o for about 20 minutes.

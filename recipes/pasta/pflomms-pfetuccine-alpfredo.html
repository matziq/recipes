Plomm’s Pfetuccine Alpfredo

Ingredients

  • 1 (16-ounce) Package Fettuccine
  • ¾ Cup Butter
  • ½ Teaspoon Garlic Powder
  • 2 Cups Whipping Cream
  • ¾ cup Grated Real Parmesan cheese, Divided
  • ¼ Cup Sour Cream

(You may add any ingredients that you’d like in order to spice up the fettuccine: mushrooms, broccoli, bacon, vegetables, chicken, etc.)

Instructions

  1. Cook fettuccine in a large dutch over (pot) according to package directions. Drain well and return cooked fettuccine to dutch oven.
  2. Melt butter in small saucepan: stir in garlic powder; add to fettuccine in dutch over. Gradually add whipping cream, ½ cup parmesan cheese and sour cream. Cook over low heat stirring constantly until thoroughly heated. Remove from heat. Transfer to a serving dish and sprinkle with remaining ¼ cup parmesan cheese. Serve immediately.

YIELDS: 6-8 Servings

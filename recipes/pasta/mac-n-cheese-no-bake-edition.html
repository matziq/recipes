Mac N Cheese (No Bake Edition)

A stovetop mac and cheese that never sees the oven. A Velveeta-and-cheddar sauce spiked with jalapeno, garlic and Sriracha gets poured hot over just-cooked pasta, melting the shredded cheddar and mozzarella tossed through it. Creamy, gooey and on the table in about the time it takes to boil noodles.

Yield: about 8 servings • Stovetop, no baking required

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pasta
  • 1/2 cup butter (for the cheese sauce)
  • 8 oz Velveeta cheese (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1/2 of a jalapeno, chopped (for the cheese sauce)
  • 2 tsp crushed garlic (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1/2 tsp pepper (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 tsp dried mustard (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (for the cheese sauce)
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, shredded (for tossing with the pasta)
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded (for tossing with the pasta)

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta to very al dente, about 4 minutes. Drain and place in a baking pan.
  2. Melt the butter and Velveeta with the chopped jalapeno and garlic in a large pan until melted. Add the Sriracha, pepper, mustard and salt, and stir to combine.
  3. Stir in the milk, cream of chicken soup, sour cream and cheddar, and combine until smooth. Heat on low for a few minutes to let the flavors all marinate together. You want your sauce to be good and hot, so it will re-heat the pasta and melt the cheese.
  4. Assemble the mac by tossing the shredded cheddar and mozzarella into the pasta, then pour the warm cheese sauce over the top.
  5. You can now place it in a hot oven for a few minutes if it needs to warm through to a gooey stage, otherwise it is ready to serve.

Notes

  • Best if eaten fresh while it is still creamy and gooey.
  • You can substitute any cheese flavors you like throughout the recipe.
  • You can also use a cream of mushroom soup if you want more of an earthy flavor.

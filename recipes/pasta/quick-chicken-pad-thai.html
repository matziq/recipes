Quick Chicken Pad Thai

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 lb. boneless, skinless chicken tenders
  • 2 tablespoons Thai Red Curry Rub (Pampered Chef)
  • 8 oz. uncooked pad Thai noodles (or linguine, or Udon noodles)
  • 2 cups unsalted chicken stock
  • 2 chopped garlic cloves
  • 4 green onions with tops
  • ½ cup prepared pad thai sauce
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • Coarsely chopped peanuts
  • Chopped fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp. olive oil in large skillet or wok, add chicken and Thai Red Curry Rub. Cook chicken 4-6 minutes, dicing with spatula as you cook into small pieces. Add stock, pasta and garlic. Bring to a simmer. Cover wok; reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer, covered 10-12 minutes or until pasta is tender. Add sauce, onions and sprouts. Garnish with peanuts, cilantro & lime wedges.

YIELDS: 6 Servings

About this dish / Did you know?

Despite their name, peanuts are legumes rather than true botanical nuts. Their pods develop underground after flowering, which is why groundnut is another common name for the same food.

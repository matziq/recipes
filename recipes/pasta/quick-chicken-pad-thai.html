Quick Chicken Pad Thai

1 tablespoon olive oil

1 lb. boneless, skinless chicken tenders

2 tablespoons Thai Red Curry Rub (Pampered Chef)

8 oz. uncooked pad Thai noodles (or linguine, or Udon noodles)

2 cups unsalted chicken stock

2 chopped garlic cloves

4 green onions with tops

½ cup prepared pad thai sauce

2 cups bean sprouts

Coarsely chopped peanuts

Chopped fresh cilantro

Lime wedges

Heat 1 tbsp. olive oil in large skillet or wok, add chicken and Thai Red Curry Rub. Cook chicken 4-6 minutes, dicing with spatula as you cook into small pieces. Add stock, pasta and garlic. Bring to a simmer. Cover wok; reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer, covered 10-12 minutes or until pasta is tender. Add sauce, onions and sprouts. Garnish with peanuts, cilantro & lime wedges.

YIELDS: 6 Servings

Jalapeño Crema

Jalapeño Crema is rich, velvety, and spicy. It’s the perfect topping for tacos or chips. Best part, it’s ready in minutes!

Yield: 1.5 cup • Prep: 5 min • Cook: 5 min • Total: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 jalapenos ((stems and seeds removed))
  • ¼ onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • ¼ bunch cilantro
  • ½ lime ((juiced))

Instructions

  1. Place the jalapenos, onion, and garlic clove in a stock pot.
  2. Cover with water.
  3. Boil for 5 minutes.
  4. Discard water. Let cool.
  5. Place the jalapenos, onion, garlic, and the rest of the ingredients in a blender.
  6. Blend until smooth.
  7. Taste for salt.
  8. Place in the fridge until ready to serve.

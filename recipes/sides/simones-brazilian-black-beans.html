Simone's Brazilian Black Beans

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups black beans
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 6 garlic cloves, finely diced
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 2 sausage links (chorizo or another preferred sausage)
  • Kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons oil for sauteing
  • Pepper to season

Instructions

  1. Soak the beans overnight, then rinse and remove small stones. Put in a pressure cooker with water 4 inches above the beans, as directed in the source. Cook 30 minutes, following the cooker's safety instructions.
  2. Saute the onion and garlic in 2 tablespoons oil. Add 4 large spoonfuls of cooked beans, mash with a fork, and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Return the skillet contents to the beans. Add sausage and cook another 15 minutes, checking doneness and water level and adding water if needed.
  4. Slice the cooked sausage and return it to the beans.

Source attribution: Simone's Brazilian Black Beans (For a pressure cooker). The ingredient list gives 6 cups water; the directions separately call for 4 inches above the beans. Both source statements are retained; no pressure setting is specified. Do not exceed your cooker's bean-fill limit.

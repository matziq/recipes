Cheesy Potato Gratin

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 pounds baking potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1 cup shredded Havarti cheese, divided
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1 cup Monterey jack cheese, divided
  • 6 tablespoons all-purpose flour, divided
  • ¼ t garlic powder
  • ¼ t cayenne pepper
  • 3 cups fat free ½ & ½
  • 2 tablespoons Chicken Flavored Bouillon Granules, divided
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese
  • ½ cup corn flakes or bread crumbs

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 400 degrees F. Spray a 9 x 13-inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray. Arrange 1/3 of the potatoes in the dish. Sprinkle with 1 teaspoon bouillon and season to taste with freshly ground pepper. Add 1/3 of the cheese and 2 tablespoons flour. Continue adding two more layers of potatoes, bouillon, pepper, flour and cheese. In bowl, combine whipping cream, remaining chicken bouillon, garlic powder and cayenne pepper. Pour mixture over the potatoes. Top with parmesan cheese and corn flakes (or bread crumbs). Bake for 60 minutes or until the top is golden brown and the potatoes are tender.

About this dish / Did you know?

The potato we eat is a tuber: a swollen part of an underground stem, rather than a root. The plant is native to the Andes, while the sweet potato is a different plant; these facts describe the ingredient, not the origin of this particular dish.

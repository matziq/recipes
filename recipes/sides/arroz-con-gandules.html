Arroz con Gandules

(rice w/garbanzo beans)

1 teaspoon olive oil

½ cup chopped onion

2 garlic cloves, minced

1 (4-ounce) boneless center-cut loin pork chop, cubed

2 cups uncooked medium-grain rice (I like brown)

1 cup water

¼ cup lean smoked ham, cubed

½ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon dried oregano

2 (14-ounce) cans beef broth

½ cup Spanish stuffed olives, chopped

2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro

1 (15.5-ounce) can garbanzo beans

Heat oil in a large dutch overn over medium-high heat. Add onion, garlic, and pork; sauté 5 minutes. Stir in rice. Add water and the next 4 ingredients (through broth); bring to boil. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 20 minutes or until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender. Stir in olives, cilantro, and the beans.

Yeild: 10 servings (3/4 cup serving size)

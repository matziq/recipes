Grilled Asparagus

Ingredients

  • 1 pound fresh asparagus
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
  •  salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat.
  2. Trim bottoms of asparagus. Lightly coat the spears with the oil and garlic. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Grill over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes, or to desired tenderness.

About this dish / Did you know?

Onions, garlic, leeks and chives belong to the same plant genus, Allium. Their related aromas explain why these plants appear so often as seasonings, despite the different shapes and textures of their bulbs and leaves.

Jeera Rice

Fragrant basmati rice tempered with cumin, ghee, and green chile.

Yield: 4 servings • Prep: 30 min • Cook: 10 min • Total: 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon ghee (or avocado oil for vegan)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 Thai green chile, finely chopped (or 1 serrano)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice in several changes of cold water until the water is mostly clear. Cover with fresh water, soak for 20 to 30 minutes, and drain before cooking.
  2. About 5 minutes before the soaking time ends, warm the ghee in a medium saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the cumin and cook for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant and sizzling.
  3. Add the chopped chile and cook for 20 seconds without letting it brown.
  4. Gently stir in the drained rice. Coat the grains in the seasoned ghee and toast for 1 to 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in the water and add the salt. Bring to a rolling boil over high heat, then reduce to medium-low, cover tightly, and simmer for 4 to 6 minutes. When the rice is tender and most of the water is absorbed, remove it from the heat and leave it covered for 2 minutes.
  6. Fluff gently with a fork, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot.

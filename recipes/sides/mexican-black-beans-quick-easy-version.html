Mexican Black Beans (quick easy version)

Ingredients

  • 2 cans black beans
  • Cumin
  • Garlic salt
  • Onion salt
  • Cayenne Pepper
  • Salsa (approximately 2 Tablespoons)
  • Queso Fresca (approximately 2 Tablespoons)
  • Optional: Jalapen0 & chopped onion

Instructions

  1. Drain and rinse one can of black beans, drain the second can as much as you can without rinsing. This leaves just a little bit of juice to make them saucy. Season to taste with cumin, onion salt, garlic salt and a dash of cayenne pepper. Add a couple of spoons of Salsa (just enough to add some flavor and sauciness). Mix and warm beans than add a couple of spoons of queso fresca.
  2. Alternatively, if you have a little extra time, heat some olive oil in a pan and cook onions, jalapenos and chopped garlic until cooked through, then add beans and flavor to taste. Add salsa & queso fresca.

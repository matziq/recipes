Red Beans with Cheese

Sides, fruit & salads · Sides

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 2 (19-ounce) cans kidney beans (about 4 cups), drained and rinsed
  • 2 (14 1/2-ounce) cans low-sodium chicken stock (about 4 cups)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 fresh jalapeno with seeds, quartered
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • Coarse salt
  • 1/4 cup grated cotija cheese

Instructions

  1. Combine beans, stock, bay leaves, onion, jalapeno, cumin, and 1/2 teaspoon salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then briskly simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Strain and keep 1/2 cup cooking liquid. Discard the onion, jalapeno, and bay leaves.
  3. Return beans and reserved liquid to the pan with 2 tablespoons cheese. Mash about half the beans, leaving the rest whole.
  4. Serve warm with the remaining 2 tablespoons cheese.

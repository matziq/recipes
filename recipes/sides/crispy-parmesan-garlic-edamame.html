Crispy Parmesan Garlic Edamame

Baked in the oven with simple ingredients, this parmesan garlic edamame is a simple, low-calorie snack that still packs a ton of flavor.

Yield: 4 servings • Prep: 5 min • Cook: 15 min • Total: 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups uncooked edamame (thawed and dried (if necessary))
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 pinch salt (to taste)
  • 1 pinch black pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper, then set aside.
  2. In a medium bowl, add Parmesan cheese, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, then mix together. Set bowl nearby.
  3. In another medium bowl, add fresh (or thawed) edamame and olive oil. Mix well until all edamame is coated.
  4. Pour Parmesan cheese mixture on top of edamame, then stir until edamame is evenly coated.
  5. Spread edamame on prepared baking sheet in an even layer.
  6. Bake in oven for 12-15 minutes or until cheese is melted and browned to your liking.
  7. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
  8. Serve as desired.

Spicy Sweet Potato Wedges

Ingredients

  • 6 medium sweet potatoes
  • Cooking Spray
  • 4 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground red pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat over to 500o
  2. Peel potatoes; cut each in cubes or lengthwise quarters. Place potatoes in a large bowl; coat with cooking spray.
  3. Combine sugar, salt, and peppers, and sprinkle over potatoes, tossing well to coat. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on a balking sheet. Bake at 500 o for 10 minutes; turn wedges over. Bake and additional 10 minutes or until tender and beginning to brown.

Yield: 8 servings

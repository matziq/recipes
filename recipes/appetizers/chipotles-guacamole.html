Chipotle Mexican Grill Spicy Guacamole

Source: Chef Staci Raymond - Chipotle Mexican Grill, Phoenix, Arizona

Ingredients

  • 1 large ripe avocado, peeled and pitted
  • 2 teaspoons fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 large cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 large Serrano chilies, seeded and chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Using fork, mash avocado with lime juice in small bowl. Add cilantro, onion, garlic, Serrano chilies and salt; stir to combine.

Makes about 1 cup.

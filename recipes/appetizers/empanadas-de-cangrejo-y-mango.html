Empanadas de Cangrejo y Mango (Crab & Mango Empanadas)

Yield: 12 empanadas

Ingredients

  • ½ cup chopped peeled mango
  • ¼ cup frozen whole‑kernel corn, thawed
  • ¼ cup chopped green onions
  • 2 tbsp fat‑free mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
  • 4 oz lump crabmeat, drained and checked for shells
  • 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced
  • Prepared Empanada Dough
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • Cooking spray

Instructions

  1. 1. Prep the oven
  2. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  3. 2. Make the filling
  4. In a bowl, combine the first 7 ingredients (mango through jalapeño).
  5. 3. Assemble the empanadas
  6. Cut dough into 12 circles using a 4‑inch cutter.
  7. Place 1 tablespoon of filling on half of each circle, leaving a ½‑inch border.
  8. Mix water and egg, brush around the edges.
  9. Fold dough over and seal with a fork or your fingers.
  10. 4. Bake
  11. Place empanadas on a baking sheet coated with cooking spray.
  12. Brush tops with remaining egg wash.
  13. Bake 25 minutes, or until golden.

Nutrition (per empanada)

169 calories; 8.1g fat; 4.8g protein; 19.2g carbs; 0.8g fiber; 28mg cholesterol; 163mg sodium

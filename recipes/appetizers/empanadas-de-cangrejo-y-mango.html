Empanadas de Cangrejo y Mango (Crab & Mango Empanadas)
Yield: 12 empanadas

Ingredients

½ cup chopped peeled mango

¼ cup frozen whole‑kernel corn, thawed

¼ cup chopped green onions

2 tbsp fat‑free mayonnaise

1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro

4 oz lump crabmeat, drained and checked for shells

1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced

Prepared Empanada Dough

1 tbsp water

1 large egg, lightly beaten

Cooking spray

Instructions

1. Prep the oven

Preheat oven to 425°F.

2. Make the filling

In a bowl, combine the first 7 ingredients (mango through jalapeño).

3. Assemble the empanadas

Cut dough into 12 circles using a 4‑inch cutter.

Place 1 tablespoon of filling on half of each circle, leaving a ½‑inch border.

Mix water and egg, brush around the edges.

Fold dough over and seal with a fork or your fingers.

4. Bake

Place empanadas on a baking sheet coated with cooking spray.

Brush tops with remaining egg wash.

Bake 25 minutes, or until golden.

Nutrition (per empanada)

169 calories; 8.1g fat; 4.8g protein; 19.2g carbs; 0.8g fiber; 28mg cholesterol; 163mg sodium

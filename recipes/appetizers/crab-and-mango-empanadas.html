Crab y Mango Empanadas

Empanada Filling

Ingredients

  • ½ cup chopped peeled mango
  • ¼ cup frozen whole-kernel corn, thawed
  • ¼ cup chopped green onions
  • 2 tablespoons fat-free or lite mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
  • 4 ounces lump crabmeat, drained
  • 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced

Empanada Dough

  • 2 cups flour
  • 6 tablespoons ice water
  • 1 teaspoon cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup vegetable shortening

Instructions

  1. Lightly spoon flour into dry measuring cups, and level with a knife. Combine ½ cup flour, water, and vinegar, stirring with a whisk until well blended to form a slurry.
  2. Combine 1 ½ cups flour, powdered sugar and salt in a bowl, stirring with a whisk; cut in shortening with a pastry blender until mixture resembles coarse meal. Add slurry; toss with a fork until flour mixture is moist.
  3. Gently press mixture into a 4-inch circle on a heavy-duty plastic wrap, and cover with additional plastic wrap. Roll the dough, still covered, into and 18 x 12-inch rectangle; freeze 10 minutes or until plastic wrap can be easily removed. Remove 2 sheets of plastic wrap, and place dough on a lightly floured surface; let stand 1 minute. Cut the Empanada Dough into 12 circles using a 4-inch biscuit cutter or glass.
  4. Combine all “filling” ingredients while empanada dough is freezing. Spoon 1 tablesppon filling onto half of each circle, leaving a ½-inch corder. Combine water and egg; brush egg mixture around edge of each circle. Fold dough over filling; press edges together with a fork or fingers to seal.
  5. Place empanadas on a baking sheet coated with cooking spray; brush with remaining egg mixture. Bake at 425o for 25 minutes or until golden.

12 servings

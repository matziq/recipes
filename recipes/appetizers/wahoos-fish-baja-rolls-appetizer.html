Wahoo’s Fish Baja Rolls Appetizer

Yield six rolls or 42 pieces

Ingredients

  • 6 12-inch tortillas (the thinner the better)
  • 9 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 12 ounces fish fillet
  • 9 ounces spinach (cleaned, with stems removed)
  • 18 ounces salsa or pico de gallo of your choice

Instructions

  1. Oven-broil or grill the fish.
  2. Lay out one tortilla and spread 1 ½ ounces of cream cheese from edge to edge. Place 1 ½ ounces of spinach down the center of the tortilla from left to right. Place 2 ounces of the fish on top of the spinach. Place 3 ounces of the salsa on top of the fish. Tightly roll the tortilla away from you. When near the end, pinch in the two ends to close them. Lightly grill the Baja Roll to brown the tortilla. Cut each roll into 7 pieces.

Note: You may substitute cooked chicken for the fish.

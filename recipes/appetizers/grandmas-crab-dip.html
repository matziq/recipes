Grandma’s Crab Dip

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (8-ounce) container sour cream
  • 1/4 cup onion, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon celery salt
  • 4 drops Tabasco sauce, or hot sauce of your choice
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1/2 pound frozen shrimp, deveined, thawed, and chopped (or more, if desired)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, beat together the cream cheese, sour cream, onion, celery salt, Tabasco sauce, garlic powder, and mayonnaise until well combined.
  2. Taste the mixture and adjust the seasonings as needed.
  3. Stir in the shrimp.
  4. Refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.
  5. Serve with chips.

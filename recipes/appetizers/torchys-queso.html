Torchy's Copycat Queso

A Creamy, Flavor-Packed Tex-Mex Dip

Rich, smooth, and utterly addictive, this copycat Torchy's queso brings Austin-style comfort right to your kitchen. A velvety blend of melted cheeses forms the base, while roasted green chiles and fresh serranoes add that signature Tex-Mex kick. Topped with fresh guacamole, crumbled queso fresco, cilantro, and a drizzle of Diablo sauce, this dip is perfect for game day gatherings or whenever the craving strikes. This recipe captures the flavors that made Torchy's famous.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 1 hr.
  • Cook Time: 20 mins
  • Servings: 10 people
  • Calories: 105 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 oz) can of whole Hatch green chiles, diced
  • 3 Roma tomatoes

Salsa Verde Ingredients

  • 1-2 serrano chili peppers (adjust to taste; use 1 for less heat or substitute with a jalapeño for a milder version)
  • 4 large tomatillos, peeled and rinsed
  • 4 large whole cloves of garlic (for roasting)
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Queso Ingredients

  • 1 large clove of garlic, minced
  • 1 cup half-and-half (plus extra to thin if needed)
  • 1 pound Velveeta Queso Blanco, cubed
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup sharp cheddar, shredded
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
  • ½ teaspoon salt


Garnishes: Hot sauce, cilantro, Cotija cheese

Instructions

Roast Your Veggies

  1. Place the tomatillos, 4 whole garlic cloves, Roma tomatoes, and serrano chili peppers on a rimmed baking sheet lined with foil. Drizzle with olive oil. Broil for about 8-10 minutes, or until the skins of the tomatoes and tomatillos split and the peppers are slightly charred. Remove the garlic cloves and flip the tomatoes, tomatillos, and chiles. Cook another 8 minutes, or until the second side is also charred.
  2. After both sides of the veggies have roasted, remove from the oven and set aside to cool with the garlic.
  3. Once cooled, peel the tomatoes and discard the skin. Slice the stems off the serrano peppers, split down the middle, and remove the seeds. Place the tomatillos, serranoes, roasted garlic, ½ teaspoon of salt, 1 tablespoon of chopped cilantro, 1 tablespoon of lime juice, and one of the Roma tomatoes in a blender (or use an immersion blender). Blend well and set aside.
  4. Dice the other 2 Roma tomatoes very finely and set aside with the Hatch green chiles.

To Make the Queso

  1. In a heavy-bottom pot, add the butter and minced garlic. Cook over medium heat for about 30 seconds, or until fragrant. Add 1 cup of half-and-half, the reserved diced tomatoes, and the Hatch green chiles. Stir until well combined, then add the cubed Queso Blanco and sharp cheddar cheese. Once the cheese has melted, add 1 tablespoon lime juice, ½ teaspoon salt, and half of the salsa verde from the blender. For a spicier queso, add all of the salsa.
  2. Let the queso sit for 1-2 hours so the flavors from the roasted veggies can infuse into the cheese sauce. It can be refrigerated and reheated as necessary with more half-and-half.

To Serve

  1. In a serving bowl, add a scoop of guacamole. Top with the prepared queso, hot sauce, cilantro, and Cotija cheese.
  2. Serve with tortilla chips!

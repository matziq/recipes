Asparagus Cheese Tart

A springtime tart of flaky puff pastry layered with Parmesan and Gruyere, topped with roasted asparagus, olive oil and fresh thyme. Serve it warm or at room temperature as an appetizer or a light lunch.

Yield: 6 servings • Oven: 400 F • Bake: about 25 to 27 min total

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp flour
  • 1 sheet (about 8 oz) frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup grated Gruyere cheese
  • 1 lb medium asparagus, trimmed
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400 F.
  2. Sprinkle flour onto a work surface, then roll out the pastry dough to a 14 x 9-inch rectangle. Trim the dough for neat edges, then transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet. Score the pastry dough lightly with a 1-inch border on all sides. Pierce the interior dough with a fork. Bake until light golden, about 10 to 12 minutes.
  3. Remove the pastry from the oven and sprinkle with the cheeses. Place the asparagus on the interior of the tart, alternating ends and tips. Drizzle the asparagus with the olive oil, scatter thyme on top and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake until the puff pastry is golden and the asparagus is tender, about 15 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Recipe courtesy of Amanda Cushman.

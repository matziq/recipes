Play Dough Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 cup salt
  • 4 cups water
  • 4 tablespoons oil
  • 1/2 cup cream of tartar

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients in a sauce pan. Cook and stir over low/medium heat until play dough is completely formed and no longer sticky. Allow to cool slightly before storing in an air tight container or zip lock bag.

*****Add one package of unsweetened powdered Kool-Aid to your playdough to give it a great smell*****

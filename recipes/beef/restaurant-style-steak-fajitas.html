Restaurant-Style Steak Fajitas

Flank steak marinates in a lime and avocado-oil mixture, then is grilled with peppers and onion and served in warm tortillas.

Yield: 4 • Prep: 40 min • Cook: 15 min • Total: 55 min

Ingredients

  • 4 limes, juice of (about 1/2 cup)
  • 1/4 cup avocado oil
  • 1 Serrano pepper, seeded and diced finely
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp agave
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped cilantro leaves
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chile powder
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1.5 pounds flank steak (can sub skirt steak)
  • 1 large onion, sliced into 1/2-inch thick rounds
  • 3 bell peppers, stemmed and seeded, cut into strips
  • 12 flour tortillas

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the lime juice, avocado oil, serrano pepper, soy sauce, agave, garlic, cilantro, cumin, and chile powder. Measure out 1/4 cup of the marinade and set it aside.
  2. Cut the steaks in half crosswise and place them in a resealable bag. Pour in the remaining marinade, seal, and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 2 hours.
  3. Heat the grill to medium-high. Remove the steak from the bag and discard that marinade. Grill the steak for 4-6 minutes per side, or to the desired doneness, then remove it and let it rest.
  4. Brush the vegetables with oil and grill them, turning occasionally, for about 8-12 minutes, until charred and softened.
  5. Warm the tortillas and wrap them in a clean dish towel to keep them warm.
  6. Slice the steak thinly against the grain and brush it with the reserved marinade. Serve with the peppers, onion, and warm tortillas.

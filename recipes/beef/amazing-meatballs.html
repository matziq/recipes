Amazing Meatballs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. lean ground beef
  • ½ cup bread crumbs (or 11 saltine cracker crumbs)
  • 1 egg
  • ½ t. onion salt
  • ½ t. garlic salt
  • 1 t Italian seasoning
  • 1 t thyme
  • ½ rosemary
  • ½ t. oregano
  • ½ t. salt

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and shape into meatballs. Place in baking dish covered in olive oil and cook for 30 minutes at 400 degrees, turning meatballs after 15 minutes of cook time.

About this dish / Did you know?

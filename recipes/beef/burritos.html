Burritos

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. lean hamburger meat
  • 2 lbs. pinto beans
  • Lime juice
  • Garlic salt
  • Onion salt
  • Cayenne pepper
  • 3 cups cheese (or to taste)
  • 30 Tortillas (they are SO much better w/the raw ones you cook yourself)

Shared assembly seasoning (specified in the method)

  • 1 package taco seasoning

Instructions

  1. Soak pinto beans overnight, then cover beans in water and bring to boil, throw out water, repeat two more time, then boil beans for 4hrs., or until soft, in plenty of water with the lid on at a low temperature then mash when done. Add a little bit of the bean juice, lime juice, onion and garlic salt and a little cayenne pepper to taste. Making beans this way tastes very YUMMY.

Ingredients

or for a faster, but not quite as yummy version…

  • Fat free refried beans – just as many cans as makes the right combo of meat and beans that you like

Instructions

  1. Cook hamburger meat in a package of taco seasoning. Add hamburger meat and cheese to beans and mix it all together. Wrap ingredients in tortillas.

This recipe makes a lot of burritos! After you have them all made, individually wrap them in aluminum foil and stick them in the freezer. They warm up quick and easy in the microwave when you are short on time. The original put together is more time consuming, but it’s nice to have them in the freezer for when you don’t have time to cook something.

About this dish / Did you know?

Tortillas provide the foundation for several distinct dishes. Tacos use a folded tortilla, burritos wrap a filling, and enchiladas are covered with sauce; the filling alone does not determine the name.

Best At Home Steak

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Steak, cut and amount unspecified|Olive oil for coating|Garlic-infused extra-virgin olive oil for basting|Sugar, small amount suggested|Seasoning blend

  • 3 tbsp coarsely ground mixed black, white, green, and pink peppercorns
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/8 tsp ground red pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 325 F. Coat steak with olive oil and seasoning; a small amount of sugar is suggested for browning.
  2. Sear in a hot skillet 2 minutes per side, then transfer to a rack over a shallow pan and baste with garlic-infused oil.
  3. Finish in the oven up to 25-30 minutes; the original calls 140 F medium.

Source limitation / note: The source gives no steak size or oil quantity, mentions reserving 2 tsp seasoning for an unspecified steak sauce, and uses a 140 F target. USDA guidance for whole beef steaks is 145 F followed by a 3-minute rest.

Attribution: Source quotes Ms. Anderson and chef Richard Chamberlain.

Source: Beef\Best At Home Steak.docx. Ingredient facts reviewed from the supplied source; directions independently summarized. Original file unchanged.

Additional source facts: Chamberlain notes that bone-in steaks cook more slowly. T-bone comes from the center of the short loin; porterhouse includes the tenderloin tip; New York strip is also called Kansas City steak; rib-eye is also called Delmonico and may be bone-in. The original offers a roughly 10-minute holding interval with the oven switched off and door ajar; that is a source suggestion, not a verified safe holding specification.

Grandma’s Yummy Meatloaf

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. lean ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 1 stack of saltine crackers
  • 2 T. dried onions
  • 1 T. salt
  • 1 T. ground black pepper
  • ¼ C. milk
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1 can tomato soup

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients except the tomato soup together and shape into a bread pan. Pour tomato soup over top of the loaf. Cook at 3500 for 60-75 minutes.

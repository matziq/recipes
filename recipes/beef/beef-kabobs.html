Beef Kabobs

Curried

1 tablespoon olive oil

1 large clove garlic, pushed through a press

2 teaspoons curry powder

1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)

1 pound boneless beef top sirloin steak, cut into 1 1/4" pieces

Salt

Montreal

1 tablespoon olive oil

1 teaspoon chopped garlic

2 tablespoons beef seasoning (Montreal or any other that sounds good)

1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar

Splash of red wine or red wine vinegar

1 teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon black pepper

Combine ingredients in a Ziplock bag; add beef. Marinate in refrigerator for 1 hour to 1 day.

Thread beef and mushrooms (or other veggies) onto metal skewers. Place kabobs on grill over medium coals. Grill 8 to 10 minutes, turning occasionally, for medium. Season to taste with salt.

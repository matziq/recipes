Easy Baja Sauce (for Tacos and more!)

This homemade Baja sauce is a creamy and flavorful taco sauce that you can make in 5 minutes. Drizzle it over shrimp or fish tacos for an easy flavor boost!

Yield: 8 • Prep: 5 min • Total: 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • ⅓ cup cotija cheese
  • 2 Tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 Tablespoon honey
  • 1 Tablespoon hot sauce (or more to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • salt (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Whisk together all the ingredients. Salt to taste and add more hot sauce as needed.
  2. Refrigerate until ready to use.

Bbq Sauce

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 4 cloves minced garlic
  • 3/4 cup Jack Daniels
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tbsp salt
  • 2 cups ketchup
  • 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp liquid smoke, optional
  • 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/3 tsp hot pepper sauce, to taste, or 1 tsp horseradish
  • Onion, mentioned in method; amount missing
  • Tomato paste, mentioned in method; amount missing

Instructions

  1. Simmer onion, garlic, and whiskey over medium heat about 10 minutes until onion is translucent.
  2. Mix in the remaining sauce ingredients; bring to a boil, reduce to medium-low, and simmer 20 minutes.

Source limitation / note: Incomplete source: onion and tomato-paste amounts are absent. A personal note says the whiskey was omitted and the sauce was not cooked; that note is not a recovered complete formula.

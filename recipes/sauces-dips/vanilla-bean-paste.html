Vanilla Bean Paste

An alcohol-based vanilla paste adapted from Lani Fox at Rosemallow Artisanal. Use the same amount as vanilla extract in recipes. A high-powered blender gives the smoothest texture; a food processor and standard blender can also be used.

Yield: About 32 oz with extracted beans, or about 20 oz with fresh beans

Ingredients

  • 8 oz extracted vanilla bean pods without seeds, weighed after extraction
  • 8 oz sugar, or 1 tightly packed cup brown sugar
  • 14 fluid oz food-grade drinking alcohol, 80-90 proof (such as dark spiced rum)
  • 2 fluid oz honey, corn syrup, or invert sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon xanthan gum (optional)

Instructions

  1. [Choose the vanilla beans]
  2. For alternatives to the 8 oz seedless extracted pods, use 4.5 oz fresh beans, 2.5 oz ground vanilla bean powder, or 7.5 oz extracted beans that still contain their seeds. Weigh extracted beans after extraction. Beans soaked for one month or less use the fresh-bean amount. For beans previously used in a paste that left the pods unground, use 5.5 oz.
  3. [High-powered blender method]
  4. Add the vanilla beans, sugar, alcohol, and honey or syrup to the blender, leaving out the optional xanthan gum. Secure the lid, then gradually raise the speed to high.
  5. Blend on high for 1 1/2-2 minutes, until the pods are finely ground.
  6. If using xanthan gum, stop the blender, add the gum gradually at low speed, then secure the lid and blend on high for another 30 seconds.
  7. [Food processor and standard blender method]
  8. Process the vanilla beans alone for 1-1 1/2 minutes. Add 2 fluid oz of the alcohol and process for another 1-1 1/2 minutes.
  9. Scrape down the bowl, add another 2 fluid oz alcohol, and process for 1-1 1/2 minutes.
  10. Scrape down the bowl and add half the sugar. Process for 1-1 1/2 minutes. Scrape again, gradually add 4 fluid oz alcohol, and process for 1-1 1/2 minutes.
  11. Scrape down the bowl, add the remaining sugar, and process for 1-1 1/2 minutes. Scrape again, add the honey or syrup, and process for another 1-1 1/2 minutes.
  12. Move the mixture to a standard blender and add the remaining 6 fluid oz alcohol. Blend for 3-7 minutes, checking about once per minute, until very smooth with minimal fibers or pieces. If using xanthan gum, blend it in as described in the high-powered method.
  13. [Storage and use]
  14. Transfer to a clean, wide-mouth jar, put on the lid, and label with the preparation date. Do not water-bath can or pressure-can this alcohol-based mixture; the source warns that doing so is dangerous and unnecessary.
  15. Rosemallow describes counter storage for several months and flavor that develops with time. This is the source's storage guidance, not a tested canning or shelf-stability process. Keep the required 80-90-proof alcohol; do not substitute a lower-proof liquid.
  16. Stir or shake before using if the paste separates, especially when the optional xanthan gum is omitted. Substitute one-for-one for vanilla extract; this is an alcohol-containing flavoring, not a ready-to-eat spread.

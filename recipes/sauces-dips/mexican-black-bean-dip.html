Mexican Black Bean Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 15.5 oz. can black beans
  • 1/3 C. medium-hot salsa
  • ¼ C. cilantro leaves
  • 4 tsp. lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic l clove, minced
  • Cayenne pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Drain and rinse the black beans. Combine the black beans, salsa, cilantro leave, lemon juice, and garlic in a food processor. Blend until smooth. Makes 1 ¼ cups and serves 4-6. Use with chips or vegetables.

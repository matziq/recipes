Guacamole

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • ½ teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1 teaspoon onion salt
  • 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and chopped (optional)
  • ¼ bunch cilantro, chopped (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cut avocado in ½, discard seed. Dice avocado inside shell and spoon into bowl. Add lime juice, garlic salt and onion salt to avocados. Use fork or pastry chopper to mash avocado to desired consistency (I like to leave mine a little chunky).
  2. Stir in jalapeno and cilantro, if desired. Add additional salt and/or lime juice to taste.

About this dish / Did you know?

Avocados were cultivated in tropical America before the Spanish conquest. The edible flesh comes from a fruit grown on a tree, even though its creamy texture makes it especially familiar in savory dishes such as guacamole.

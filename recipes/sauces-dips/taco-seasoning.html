Taco Seasoning

Taco seasoning is easy to make at home, plus, it's cheaper than store-bought blends and tastier, too! Learn how to make a big batch to store in your pantry.

Yield: 1 serving(s) • Prep: 2 min • Total: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp. chili powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp. ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp. paprika
  • 1/2 tsp. crushed red pepper
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 tsp. garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp. onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp. dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp. black pepper

Instructions

  1. Measure out all the ingredients in a small bowl. Mix well.
  2. Taste and adjust the salt or spices as desired. Store in an airtight container until ready to use.

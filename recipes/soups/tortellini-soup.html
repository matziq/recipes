Tortellini Soup

Ingredients

  • 2 cloves crushed garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cans or 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 pkg fresh or frozen tortellini
  • 15 oz can spinach
  • 14 ½ oz can diced stewed tomatoes
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. In a large soup pot, cook garlic and oil over medium high heat for 2-3 minutes. Add broth and tortellini. Heat to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes. Add spinach and tomatoes. Simmer 5 minutes. Serve with parmesan cheese.

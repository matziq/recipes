Slow-Cooker Chicken Tortilla Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 pound raw or frozen chicken breasts
  • 1 (15 ounce) fire roasted diced tomatoes
  • 1 (10 ounce) can enchilada sauce (red)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 (4 ounce) can chopped green chile peppers
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (I use the chopped garlic in a jar)
  • 2 lb box chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 (10 ounce) package frozen corn ( I omit the corn and use black beans)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
  • 7 corn tortillas
  • vegetable oil
  • lime juice

Instructions

  1. Place chicken, tomatoes, enchilada sauce, onion, green chiles, and garlic into a slow cooker. Pour in chicken stock, and season with cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper, and bay leaf. Stir in corn and cilantro. Cover, and cook on Low setting for 6 to 8 hours or on High setting for 3 to 4 hours.
  2. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  3. Lightly brush both sides of tortillas with oil and lime juice and salt to taste. Cut tortillas into strips, then spread on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake in preheated oven until crisp, about 10 to 15 minutes. To serve, sprinkle tortilla strips over soup.
  5. Garnish soup with some or all: cheese, sour cream, avocado, lime, cilantro

Source note: Food safety: thaw frozen chicken before slow cooking and verify it reaches 165 F. This differs from the source's allowance for frozen chicken.

Pablano Soup

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 ½ large carrots – diced
  • 1 large onions – diced
  • 1 ½ stalks celery – diced
  • 1 large potatoes – diced
  • 1 tablespoons diced garlic
  • 1 pablano peppers, seeded & diced
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoons white pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon tyme
  • 1 ½ tablespoons chicken bullion
  • 2 quarts chicken broth
  • ¼ bunch cilantro – minced
  • 1 ½ cups chicken – diced
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ¾ cup flour
  • ½ teaspoon tabasco sauce
  • ½ cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Heat oil – cook veggies & spices. Saute until softened
  2. Add broth and cook for 5 minutes
  3. Add potatoes
  4. Cook another 5ish minutes or until veggies are tender
  5. Melt butter, add flour & 1 cup or more of broth from soup. Add to soup & stir until thick.
  6. Remove from heat and add hot sauce & cream
  7. Enjoy!

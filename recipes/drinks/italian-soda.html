Italian Soda

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp half-and-half
  • 3 1/2 tbsp syrup
  • Ice, amount unspecified
  • Carbonated water, amount unspecified
  • Whipped cream, amount unspecified

Instructions

  1. Measure syrup into a glass, then add ice, half-and-half, and carbonated water.
  2. Stir and top with whipped cream.

About this dish / Did you know?

Carbonation means adding carbon dioxide to a drink, which gives sparkling water and soda their bubbles and tang. Here the source starts with carbonated water, so the fizz is separate from the syrup and dairy flavorings.

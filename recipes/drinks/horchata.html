Horchata

A refreshing rice and almond drink with cinnamon and vanilla, served over ice.

Yield: 7 cups | Prep: 10 minutes, plus at least 6 hours soaking (or overnight) | Cook: None

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (200g) uncooked white rice, jasmine recommended
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 cup (43g) slivered almonds
  • 1 can (12 oz / 340g) evaporated milk
  • 2/3 cup (132g) granulated sugar, plus more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon aged vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups (360ml) almond milk
  • 4 1/4 cups (1 liter) water, plus additional water for rinsing and soaking
  • Ice, for serving
  • Cinnamon sticks, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse under cold running water until the water runs clear.
  2. Put the rice, cinnamon stick, and almonds in a medium bowl. Add enough water to cover, soak for at least 6 hours or overnight, then drain.
  3. Transfer the drained rice, cinnamon, and almonds to a blender. Add the evaporated milk and sugar, then blend on high for 3-5 minutes until smooth.
  4. Pour through cheesecloth into a pitcher. Mix in the vanilla and almond milk, then stir in the measured 4 1/4 cups (1 liter) of water.
  5. Taste and add more sugar if desired. Pour over ice and add a cinnamon stick to each glass if you like.

Notes

For a dairy-free variation, replace the evaporated milk with canned sweetened coconut cream. Adjust the added sugar to taste because the coconut cream is sweetened; evaporated milk is not sweetened condensed milk.

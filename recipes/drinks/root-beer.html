Root Beer

5 lbs sugar

4 gallons water

2 oz. root beer extract

5 lbs dry ice

Directions:

Make 2 hours before serving!

Pour 2 ½ gallons of water in got, along with sugar and extract. Stir and add dry ice. Let bubble for 30 minutes.

Add remaining water and let stand for 1 ½ more hours.

Serve & enjoy!

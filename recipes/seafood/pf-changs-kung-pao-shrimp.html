P. F. Chang's China Bistro Kung Pao Shrimp

Source: Chef Stephen Yahwak of PF Chang's China Bistro

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 3 ounces Kung Pao sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon Crushed Red Pepper
  • 8 ounces shrimp
  • 1 tablespoon chopped green onion
  • 5 whole chile pods
  • Sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Heat wok (smoking), introduce oil and shrimp, separate and velvet for 1 to 2 minutes. Do not overcook. Carefully pour shrimp and oil onto oil skimmer. Stir fry chile pods and onions; add garlic and Kung Pao sauce. Add shrimp, toss, and add peanuts and chile flakes.

About this dish / Did you know?

Onions, garlic, leeks and chives belong to the same plant genus, Allium. Their related aromas explain why these plants appear so often as seasonings, despite the different shapes and textures of their bulbs and leaves.

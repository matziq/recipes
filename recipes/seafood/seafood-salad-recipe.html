Seafood Salad Recipe

The perfect Publix copycat seafood salad or sandwich shop seafood salad recipe! Reader favorite recipe!

Yield: 6 • Prep: 15 min • Total: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1- 1.5 lb imitation crab, flake style
  • 6 oz can of tiny shrimp (optional, I never use these)
  • 2-3 green onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 celery stalks (diced)
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise (I use Hellmann's)
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/8 tsp dried dill (or to taste)
  • salt & pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Remove imitation crab from the package and separate the flakes.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, chop and shred the crab.
  3. Add all in ingredients and mix well.
  4. Refrigerate for 1-2 hours or overnight to blend flavors.
  5. Serve with crackers for a snack, on deli rolls for a crab salad sandwich, or mixed in with pasta for a great pasta salad dish!

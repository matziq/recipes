Rabago’s Shrimp Cocktail

Ingredients

  • 8 Romano tomatoes
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 3 pounds shrimp
  • ½ bottle ketchup
  • 1 small chopped onion
  • Lemon Juice to cover cooked shrimp
  • Louisiana Sauce to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large saute pan, melt the butter. Stir in the brown sugar and cinnamon. Add the bananas and baste them with the syrup for 2 minutes. Pull the saute pan off the burner. Carefully add the banana liqueur and rum. Place the pan back on the stove and shake the pan a couple of times. The pan should flame, if not, ignite the pan with a match. Shake the pan back and forth until the flame dies, basting the bananas continuously. Remove from the heat

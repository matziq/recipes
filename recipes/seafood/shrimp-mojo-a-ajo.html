Shrimp Mojo A Ajo

2 dozen jumbo shrimp, with shells

Olive oil

Salt

4 tablespoons butter

4 tablespoons olive oil

10 garlic cloves, cut in chunks

1 tablespoon lime juice

Fresh shopped cilantro for garnish

Preheat the broiler. Butterfly the shrimp, with shells on, by cutting along inside curve and then pressing flat. Brush all over with olive oil and sprinkle with salt. Broil, shells up, for 4 minutes.

Make the sauce by melting butter with oil in small pan over low heat. Add garlic and salt and gently cook, swirling pan occasionally, until garlic is golden, about 7 minutes. Swirl in lime juice and remove from heat.

Arrange shrimp, split side up, on serving plates. Spoon on garlic sauce, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot.

Serves 4

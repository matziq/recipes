Shrimp Braised in Oil

(makes 4 servings)

1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil

4 big cloves garlic (peeled and cut into thin slivers)

1 tsp cumin (or to taste)

1 tsp paprika (or to taste)

2 lb shrimp of 15 or 20 pound

Salt and freshly ground black pepper

¼ cup chopped parsley

Combine the oil and garlic in a 10 or 12 inch skillet. Turn the heat to medium, and cook until the garlic begins to sizzle, then add the cumin and paprika. Stir, raise the heat to medium-high, and add the shrimp. Season with salt and pepper.

Cook, stirring occasionally, until the shrimp are all just pink, but no more than that. Do not evaporate their liquid. Turn off the heat, add the parsley, and serve with crusty bread.

VARIATION:

To make the shrimp Roman-style, omit cumin and paprika and substitute cayenne for black pepper. When the shrimp are cooked, stir in 2 or more tablespoons of freshly squeezed lemon juice. Garnish with parsley, and serve with lemon wedges.

For shrimp with Asian flavors, substitute peanut or vegetable oil for olive oil and cook 1 tablespoon of chopped ginger and two or three dried red chilies (or to taste) along with the garlic. Omit cumin and paprika. When the shrimp are cooked, stir in 1 tablespoon of soy sauce, then garnish with minced scallions or cilantro and serve with lime wedges.

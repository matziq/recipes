Shrimp Fajitas

Seasoned shrimp cook quickly in a skillet with bell peppers and onion, then are served with cilantro, lime, and tortillas.

Yield: 4 • Prep: 10 min • Cook: 15 min • Total: 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 2 cups sliced bell peppers (red, orange, yellow or a combination)
  • 1 onion (thinly sliced)
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • 1 pound large shrimp (peeled and deveined, tails removed if desired)
  • lime wedges for serving (optional)
  • flour tortillas and fajita toppings of your choice
  • salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over high heat. Add the bell peppers and onion and cook, stirring now and then, until tender with charred edges. Season the vegetables with salt and pepper.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Add the shrimp to the pan and sprinkle the seasoning mixture over the shrimp and vegetables. Stir to coat.
  4. Cook for 4-5 minutes, until the shrimp are pink and opaque.
  5. Scatter cilantro over the fajita mixture and serve with lime wedges, flour tortillas, and toppings of your choice.

About this dish / Did you know?

Onions, garlic, leeks and chives belong to the same plant genus, Allium. Their related aromas explain why these plants appear so often as seasonings, despite the different shapes and textures of their bulbs and leaves.

Energy Bars

Ingredients

  • 2 cups quick-cooking oats
  • 1 cup chopped nuts
  • 1/3 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 cup craisins
  • 1 cup dried fruit (apples, peaches, blueberries)
  • 1 cup of your favorite cereal

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 degrees. In an 8- or 9-inch square pan, combine oats and nuts. Bake 20 minutes or until oats are toasted golden brown, stirring occasionally.
  2. Meanwhile, combine butter, brown sugar and honey in small saucepan. Cook over medium heat until mixture boils. Pour over oat mixture. Add craisins, dried fruit and cereal. Toss to coat. With lightly floured hands, press firmly into pan. Bake at 350 degrees for 10 minutes. Cool completely. Cut into bars.

About this dish / Did you know?

Finger Gelatin (Jell-O Kitchen)

Ready

Yield: One 13 by 9-inch pan, cut into small squares

Ingredients

  • 3 packages (3 ounces each) raspberry-flavored Jell-O gelatin
  • 4 envelopes (1/4 ounce each) Knox unflavored gelatin
  • 4 cups boiling water
  • Cooking spray for the pan

Instructions

  1. Mix both dry gelatins in a large bowl. Add the boiling water and stir until no undissolved gelatin remains.
  2. Lightly spray a 13 by 9-inch pan and pour in the dissolved gelatin.
  3. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour and until firm, then cut into small squares.

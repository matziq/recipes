Complete substitute in this collection: Finger Gelatin (Jell-O Kitchen). Use its complete formula as a separate recipe, not as evidence that the original missing details were recovered.

Evidence and rationale: Manufacturer's original uses 9 ounces flavored gelatin and 4 cups boiling water, which scale exactly by one-third to the card's 3 ounces and 1 1/3 cups. It uses 4 envelopes Knox by weight; the card measures 1 1/3 tablespoons, whose weight was not verified. Manufacturer evidence was preferred to an unsupported five-star rating.

Confidence: High for dissolving/chilling sequence; moderate for final firmness because the card's tablespoon gelatin measurement is not confirmed equivalent to envelopes.

Source: Jell-O Kitchen, Kraft Heinz, Finger Gelatin. Evidence checked November 7, 2026; ratings may change.

Suggested missing method: combine the flavored and unflavored gelatins, stir in the card's boiling water until fully dissolved, then pour into a lightly sprayed pan. The full manufacturer's batch uses 13 by 9 inches; an approximately 8 by 5-inch pan is an editorial one-third-area approximation, not a manufacturer-specified pan. Alternatively use a container that gives the thickness you want. Refrigerate at least 1 hour and until firm before cutting into squares. Do not replace the card's tablespoon measure silently; follow the imported weight/envelope formula if reliable firmness is essential.

CHICKEN LUAU

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Chicken tenders
  • 2 packages thawed chopped spinach
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Onion chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Combine the ingredients in a baking dish, pouring the coconut milk over the top. Bake about 1 hour till the chicken is done. Thicken with a paste of cornstarch and water. Season with salt as needed.

About this dish / Did you know?

Coconut milk is made from the coconut's grated flesh mixed with water; it is not the clear coconut water found inside the fruit. The same fruit also provides flesh that can be eaten fresh or dried, explaining its different forms in cooking.

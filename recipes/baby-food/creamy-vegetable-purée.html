Creamy Vegetable Purée

**Makes 6 portions **Suitable for freezing

Strong-tasting root vegetable purées, such as parsnip or carrot, may be made milder with the addition of baby rice cereal. Baby cereal also combines well with steamed and puréed broccoli and cauliflower.

1 Tbsp. sugar-free, vitamin and iron-enriched baby rice cereal

3 Tbsp. breast milk or formula

4 Tbsp. first vegetable purée

Mix together the baby rice cereal and milk according to the instructions on the box, and stir into the vegetable purée until thoroughly combined.

Pour purée into an ice-cube tray and freeze.

Favorite Pancakes

The good old-fashioned buttermilk kind, from the classic Gold Medal flour cookbook. Light, tangy and reliable, with variations for sweet milk and blueberry pancakes.

Yield: 10 four-inch pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp. vegetable oil or melted shortening
  • 1 cup Gold Medal flour
  • 1 tbsp. sugar
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp. soda
  • 1/2 tsp. salt

Instructions

  1. Blend egg, milk and oil. Measure flour by the dip-level-pour method or by sifting.
  2. Blend the dry ingredients together. Add to the liquids; beat with a rotary beater until all the flour is moistened. (Batter will be slightly lumpy.)
  3. Grease a heated griddle, if necessary. To test, sprinkle with drops of water. When the water sizzles, the griddle heat is just right.
  4. Pour batter from a pitcher or the tip of a large spoon in pools slightly apart (for perfectly round cakes).
  5. Turn the pancakes as soon as they are puffed and full of bubbles, but before the bubbles break. Turn and brown on the other side.
  6. Serve immediately with butter and syrup. If necessary, keep the pancakes hot by placing them between the folds of a warm towel in a warm oven. (Don't stack them!)

Notes

  • Sweet Milk Pancakes: Make Favorite Pancakes, except substitute sweet milk for the buttermilk, add 2 tsp. more baking powder and omit the soda. For extra lightness, beat the egg yolk, add the milk, etc., then fold in the stiffly beaten egg white.
  • Blueberry Pancakes: Make Favorite Pancakes, except add 1/2 cup drained fresh or frozen berries to the batter, folding them in carefully at the last.
  • Ferry Farm Sauce (as it was made by George Washington's mother): Heat together slowly over hot water 1 cup strained honey and 1/2 cup maple syrup. Remove from heat and blend in 1 tsp. cinnamon.
  • To make pancakes thick or thin as desired: add a little more liquid to thin them; make them thicker by adding a little more flour.

Mom’s Waffles

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 teaspoon soda
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons oil

For Richer Waffles:

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 teaspoon soda
  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup oil

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and cook in waffle iron.

About this dish / Did you know?

Baking powder and baking soda can raise a batter by releasing carbon dioxide rather than waiting for yeast fermentation. They work differently and are not automatic one-for-one substitutes, so the leavener specified in this recipe matters.

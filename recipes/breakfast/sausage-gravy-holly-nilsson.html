Sausage Gravy (Holly Nilsson)

Ready

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground pork breakfast sausage
  • 1 tablespoon salted butter or bacon fat
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/3 cups whole milk, adjusted as needed for consistency
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Brown the sausage over medium-high heat in a large skillet, breaking it into small pieces. Leave its rendered fat in the pan.
  2. Melt the butter or bacon fat into the sausage. Stir in the flour and cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the milk gradually while stirring to keep the gravy smooth.
  4. Bring to a bubbling boil, then reduce to medium-low and simmer until thick. Adjust with a little milk if necessary.
  5. Season to taste and serve hot with biscuits or potatoes.

Food-safety addition: ground pork should reach 160 F. This is a full 1-pound-sausage recipe, not the half-batch adaptation on Red Door Skillet.

Recipe source: Holly Nilsson, Spend With Pennies, Sausage Gravy. Reviewed November 7, 2026.

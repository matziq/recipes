Pumpkin Pancakes with Nutella Syrup

Blender pumpkin pancakes with whole wheat flour and oats, warmly spiced with cinnamon, allspice, ginger and nutmeg, served under a drizzle of warm Nutella syrup.

Yield: about 16 pancakes • Tools: measuring tools, blender, griddle, spatula

Ingredients

  • 1 c. pumpkin
  • 1 c. whole wheat flour
  • 1 c. white flour
  • 1/2 c. oats
  • 2 c. milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 T. coconut oil
  • 2 T. vinegar
  • 3 T. brown sugar
  • 2 t. baking powder
  • 1 t. baking soda
  • 1 t. allspice
  • 1 t. cinnamon
  • 1 t. ground ginger
  • 1/2 t. nutmeg
  • 1/2 t. salt

Instructions

  1. Stir the vinegar into the milk and let it stand 5 minutes to sour; this stands in for buttermilk.
  2. Add the soured milk, pumpkin, egg, melted coconut oil, brown sugar and oats to a blender. Blend until smooth.
  3. Add the whole wheat flour, white flour, baking powder, baking soda, allspice, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg and salt. Blend just until combined; do not overmix.
  4. Heat a griddle over medium heat and grease it lightly. Pour the batter in pools and cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, about 2 to 3 minutes. Flip with a spatula and cook 1 to 2 minutes more.
  5. Serve warm with Nutella syrup.

Notes

  • Nutella syrup: warm 1/2 cup Nutella with 1/4 cup milk or cream over low heat, whisking until smooth and pourable. Thin with a splash more milk if needed.
  • The original meal-plan card listed the ingredients and tools only; the method above is the standard blender technique for this batter.

About this dish / Did you know?

Rice is the edible grain of a grass, and it is used far beyond a plain side dish. It can also be ground into flour or made into noodles and drinks, so rice-based recipes can have very different textures and roles at the table.

Ingredients

  • Sticky rice, amount unspecified
  • Cool water for soaking, 2-3 inches above rice
  • 6-8 cups water for steaming

Instructions

  1. Rinse rice 2-3 times, until water runs clear
  2. Place rinsed rice in a bowl and fill with cool water so the water is approximately 2-3 inches above the rice…let the rice stand in water for 6-8 hours
  3. Drain the rice, place it in cheesecloth, wrap it up and put the cheesecloth inside bamboo steamer. Put 6-8 cups of water in sticky rice steamer and bring to a boil. Then place bamboo steamer inside sticky rice steamer. Be sure the bottom of the bamboo steamer does not touch the boiling water. Place a standard 8 inch lid loosely over the top of the bamboo steamer.
  4. Steam the rice for 45 minutes (or until tender)
  5. Enjoy!

Fruit Pizza

Crust:

3/4 C. Butter/Margarine

1 C. Sugar

2 Eggs

1 tsp. Vanilla

2 1/2 C. Flour

1 tsp. Baking Powder

1 tsp. Salt

Mix and press out on greased pizza pan ( 1 large or 2 medium).  Bake at 375 degrees for 10 minutes.  Cool crust.

Filling:

1 (8 oz.) Pkg. Cream Cheese

1/2 C. Sugar

1/2 C. Sour Cream

1/2 tsp. Vanilla

Mix and spread over crust. 

Top with assorted fruits (kiwi, strawberry, pineapple, berries, banana, mandarin oranges, pie fillings, etc.).

Glaze:

1/2 C. Pineapple juice

2 Tbsp. Corn Starch

1 tsp. lemon juice

1/2 C. Water

1/2 C. Sugar

Boil together until thickened and pour over fruit.  Chill.

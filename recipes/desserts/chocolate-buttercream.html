Chocolate Buttercream

Chocolate Buttercream

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2-3 tablespoons heavy cream or milk
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Beat butter on medium speed until creamy and smooth (2-3 minutes).
  2. Add cocoa powder and mix until combined.
  3. Gradually add powdered sugar, 1 cup at a time, mixing on low speed.
  4. Add cream, vanilla, and salt.
  5. Beat on medium-high for 2-3 minutes until light and fluffy.

Tip: Sift cocoa powder for the smoothest buttercream.

Homemade Ice Cream

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • 3 cups sugar
  • 1 quart ½ & ½
  • 3 tablespoons of flavoring
  • 1 can evaporated milk

Instructions

  1. Fluff eggs and sugar together very well
  2. Add canned milk, ½ & ½, and flavoring and mix well
  3. Pour into ice cream freezer
  4. Top off freezer with whole milk to fill line

Ingredients

ICE & SALT for freezer

  • ~10 pounds ice
  • ~2 cups salt

Instructions

  1. Crush ice in a blender. To prepare an ice-salt bath layer about 3 inches of ice, then sprinkle 1/3 cup table salt over the ice. Continue alternating layers of ice and salt until the tub is full. Freezing should take approximately 20 minutes.

Source note: Food safety: this is an uncooked egg recipe; use pasteurized eggs. Can size and freezer capacity are not specified in the source.

About this dish / Did you know?

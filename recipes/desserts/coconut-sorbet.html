Coconut Sorbet

Prep: about 30 minutes, plus 24 hours of freezing   Dairy-free

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full-fat coconut milk
  • 100 ml coconut water
  • 50 g white sugar or agave syrup
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 1 pinch fine salt

Directions

  • Warm the coconut milk and sugar over low heat, stirring only until the sweetener dissolves. Do not let it boil. Remove from the heat and cool for about 5 minutes.
  • Blend the sweetened coconut milk with the coconut water, lime juice, and salt for about 30 seconds, until completely smooth.
  • Pour into a Ninja Creami or Ninja Swirl container and freeze it on a level surface for 24 hours.
  • Place the frozen container in the machine and process with the Sorbet program.

Variation: Blend in a few fresh mint leaves for a brighter, more refreshing flavor.

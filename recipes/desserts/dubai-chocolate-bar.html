Dubai Chocolate Bar

Makes: 2 large bars   Time: about 20 minutes, plus chilling

Ingredients

  • 150 g dried kataifi pastry, chopped
  • 52 g unsalted butter
  • 290 g pistachio cream
  • 1 tablespoon tahini
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 450 g dark or milk chocolate

Directions

  • Melt the butter in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the kataifi and cook, stirring now and then, until evenly crisp and golden, about 10 minutes.
  • Combine the pistachio cream, tahini, and salt in a bowl. Fold in the toasted kataifi until the strands are thoroughly coated.
  • Gently melt the chocolate in a double boiler or in short microwave intervals, stirring frequently. Temper it if you want the bars to have the glossiest finish and cleanest snap.
  • Spoon most of the chocolate into two bar molds. Tilt the molds to coat the bottoms and sides, pour out the excess, and chill until firm.
  • Press the pistachio-kataifi mixture into each shell, leaving enough room at the top for a final chocolate layer.
  • Cover the filling with the remaining chocolate. Level the surface with a spatula and remove any excess around the edges.
  • Chill until completely set, then carefully release the bars from their molds.

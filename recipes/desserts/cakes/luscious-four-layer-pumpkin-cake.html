Luscious Four-Layer Pumpkin Cake

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 1 package yellow cake mix, 2-layer style
  • 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin, divided
  • 1 egg, beaten (separately listed in source)
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/3 vegetable oil, unit missing
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice, divided
  • 1 package (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tub (8 oz) whipped topping, thawed
  • 1/4 cup caramel topping
  • 1/2 cup pecans

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 F; grease and flour two 9-inch round pans.
  2. Mix cake mix, 1 cup pumpkin, milk, oil, eggs, and 1 tsp spice. Divide between pans and bake 20-22 minutes; check with a toothpick. Cool completely.
  3. Beat cream cheese with powdered sugar, the remaining pumpkin and remaining spice; fold in whipped topping.
  4. Split each cake into two layers. Fill between layers, leaving the top unfrosted; add caramel and pecans. Refrigerate.

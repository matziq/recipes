White Chocolate Raspberry Cake

A doctored white cake mix enriched with sour cream, buttermilk and white chocolate pudding, studded with white chocolate chips and layered with raspberries. A handwritten family recipe card.

Yield: 1 bundt cake or 2 nine-inch layers • Oven: 325 F • Bake: 45 to 50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 egg whites
  • 1/3 c. oil
  • 2 tsp. vanilla (Mexican clear)
  • 3/4 c. buttermilk
  • 1 white cake mix (Duncan Hines)
  • 1 (3.3 oz.) pkg. white chocolate Jell-O instant pudding (not cook 'n' serve)
  • 1/2 c. white chocolate chips
  • 3/4 c. sour cream

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325 F. Grease and flour a bundt pan or two 9-inch round pans.
  2. Beat the egg whites, oil, vanilla, buttermilk and sour cream together until smooth.
  3. Add the dry cake mix and the dry pudding mix. Beat about 2 minutes, scraping the bowl, until the batter is smooth and thick.
  4. Fold in the white chocolate chips.
  5. Pour into the prepared pan and bake 45 to 50 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack.
  6. Fill or top with raspberries or raspberry preserves before serving.

Notes

  • The recipe card lists the ingredients only; the method above is the standard approach for this style of doctored cake mix.
  • For layers, spread raspberry preserves between the cooled rounds and frost with white chocolate or cream cheese buttercream.

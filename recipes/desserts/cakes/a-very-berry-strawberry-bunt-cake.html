A Very Berry Strawberry Bunt Cake

Desserts · / Cakes

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 packaged white cake mix
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 package strawberry Jell-O
  • 4 eggs
  • 8-10 oz frozen strawberries with juice

Icing

  • 7-10 large strawberries
  • 4 tbsp sugar, to taste
  • 1/4 cup water, or more if needed

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 F; grease and flour a Bundt pan.
  2. Beat cake mix, oil, eggs, and gelatin until smooth. Stir in frozen strawberries and juice.
  3. Bake about 40 minutes, checking with a toothpick.
  4. Blend the icing strawberries, sugar, and water. Poke holes in the cake with a fork and pour on the blended icing.

To Die For Carrot Cake

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Cake, group 1

  • 1 1/4 cups unsweetened applesauce or oil
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3 room-temperature eggs

Cake, group 2

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Cake, group 3

  • 2 cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup sweetened shredded coconut
  • 1 cup chopped nuts, optional
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup crushed pineapple in juice, undrained

Frosting

  • 1/2 cup softened butter
  • 8 oz softened cream cheese
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 lb powdered sugar
  • Toasted pecans or coconut for topping, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 F. Combine group 1, then mix in group 2; stir in group 3.
  2. Use a lightly greased 9-by-13-inch pan, two 9-inch rounds, or three 8-inch rounds; parchment on the bottom helps release the cake.
  3. Bake 35-40 minutes for the 9-by-13-inch or 9-inch pans, or 25-30 minutes for 8-inch pans. Check with a toothpick.
  4. Cool in pans 10 minutes, then transfer to racks and cool completely.
  5. Beat frosting butter and cream cheese together, then beat in vanilla and powdered sugar until smooth.

Source limitation / note: Source yield: 16 slices. Its 25-minute metadata is not the bake time for every pan size.

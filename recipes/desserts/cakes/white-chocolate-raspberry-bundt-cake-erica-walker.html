About this dish / Did you know?

The familiar Bundt shape comes from a fluted ring pan trademarked by Nordic Ware in 1950. Its popularity grew after a Tunnel of Fudge cake took second place in the 1966 Pillsbury Bake-Off; that is pan history, not the origin of this particular recipe.

Yield: 1 standard Bundt cake

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 box (15.25 ounces) white cake mix
  • 1 package (3.4 ounces) instant white chocolate pudding mix, dry
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 4 large whole eggs
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips, chopped smaller
  • 1/2 cup red raspberry preserves, divided
  • Cooking spray for the pan

Cream cheese frosting

  • 16 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 3-4 cups powdered sugar, adjusted to frosting consistency
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla

Instructions

  1. Preheat to 350 F and thoroughly grease a standard Bundt pan.
  2. Combine the dry cake and pudding mixes with sour cream, eggs, water, and oil. Stir the chopped chips into the batter.
  3. Put half the batter in the pan. Add 1/4 cup preserves in small dollops and swirl through the batter. Add the remaining batter and repeat with the other 1/4 cup preserves.
  4. Bake for 45-50 minutes, checking that the center is cooked. Let the cake rest in its pan for 10 minutes, then turn it out.
  5. Cool, cover, and refrigerate overnight before frosting.
  6. Beat the softened cream cheese and butter together. Add vanilla and powdered sugar gradually until the frosting is thick and spreadable, using the listed 3-4 cups as the quantity range.
  7. Spread or pipe the frosting onto the chilled cake.

This is a separate whole-egg recipe, not the existing egg-white/buttermilk family card. Do not combine their ratios.

The publisher says a 3.9-ounce pudding package may be used by measuring 1/2 cup loosely packed dry mix when a 3.4-ounce package is unavailable.

Keep the Bundt pan no more than 2/3-3/4 full. Elevation can affect baking; this import preserves the base formula rather than adding unverified altitude changes.

The source frosting method starts sugar additions at 2 cups; its ingredient list specifies 3-4 cups. Add gradually within the listed range until suitable for piping. Food-safety addition: refrigerate the cream-cheese-frosted cake.

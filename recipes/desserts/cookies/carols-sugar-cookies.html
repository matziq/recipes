Carol's Sugar Cookies

Desserts · / Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 6 cups flour
  • 2 tsp soda
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Cream butter with sugar; mix in eggs, then vanilla and buttermilk. Add the dry ingredients.
  2. Chill overnight and roll 1/4 inch thick.
  3. Bake at 375 F about 8 minutes, until bottoms are golden; cool, frost, and decorate.

About this dish / Did you know?

The word cookie comes from the Dutch koekje, meaning a little cake. Rolled, dropped and bar cookies are different ways of shaping this broad baking family, rather than names for one fixed dough.

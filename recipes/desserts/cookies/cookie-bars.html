About this dish / Did you know?

The word cookie comes from the Dutch koekje, meaning a little cake. Rolled, dropped and bar cookies are different ways of shaping this broad baking family, rather than names for one fixed dough.

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks salted butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp cornstarch
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 3/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1 bag milk chocolate chips, size unspecified

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugars with vanilla and eggs in the mixing bowl.
  2. Mix in the remaining ingredients and spread evenly in a greased Texas Brownies pan.
  3. Bake at 375 F for 20-30 minutes, until evenly golden.

Source limitation / note: Source label: half batch. Pan dimensions and chip bag size are not given.

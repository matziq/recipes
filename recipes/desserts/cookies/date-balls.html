Date Balls

Desserts · / Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks butter
  • 2 small packages chopped dates
  • 2 cups chopped pecans
  • 10x powdered sugar, amount unspecified
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 1 small package coconut flakes
  • 4 cups Rice Krispies

Instructions

  1. Heat butter and brown sugar in a heavy pan until sugar melts; add dates and coconut and cook 6 minutes.
  2. Off heat, stir in pecans and cereal, adding the cereal in two portions.
  3. Roll into small balls and coat in powdered sugar.

About this dish / Did you know?

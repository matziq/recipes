Pecan Coconut Triangles

Desserts · / Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Crust

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup butter

Filling

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup chopped pecans
  • 3/4 cup coconut
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 tbsp light cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix crust flour, sugar, and salt; cut in butter until crumbly. Press into a 9-by-13-inch pan; bake at 350 F 10 minutes.
  2. Beat eggs lightly and stir in brown sugar, pecans, honey, and melted butter. Mix cream with vanilla and stir into filling.
  3. Spread over hot crust and bake 20 more minutes until set. Cool on a rack; cut squares diagonally into triangles.

Source limitation / note: Coconut is listed in the filling but the original method does not say when to add it; no mixing step is invented. Dip the cutting knife in hot water and wipe between cuts.

About this dish / Did you know?

Coconut milk is made from the coconut's grated flesh mixed with water; it is not the clear coconut water found inside the fruit. The same fruit also provides flesh that can be eaten fresh or dried, explaining its different forms in cooking.

Coconut Chews

Desserts · / Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup flour
  • Pinch salt

Topping

  • 1 1/2 cups brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup flaked coconut
  • 1 cup chopped nuts

Instructions

  1. Cut butter into flour, granulated sugar, and salt. Press into the source's 9.13 baking pan and bake at 350 F for 15 minutes.
  2. Beat brown sugar with eggs; add vanilla, coconut, and nuts. Spread over crust and bake 25 more minutes.
  3. Cut while warm; cool before covering.

Source limitation / note: The printed pan dimension is '9.13'; it is not silently interpreted or corrected.

About this dish / Did you know?

Coconut milk is made from the coconut's grated flesh mixed with water; it is not the clear coconut water found inside the fruit. The same fruit also provides flesh that can be eaten fresh or dried, explaining its different forms in cooking.

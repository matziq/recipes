Butter Toffee Bars

Gooey, blondie-like bars filled with English toffee bits. These buttery treats are also called brickle bars.

Yield: 24 bars | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes | Cool: A few minutes before cutting

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter, softened, plus extra to grease the pan
  • 1 1/2 cups (180g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 cup (198g) granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup (107g) packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon aged vanilla extract
  • 1 bag (8 oz / 227g) English toffee bits

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F (175 C). Butter a 9x13-inch (23x33 cm) baking pan.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder, and salt into a bowl and set aside.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat the softened butter and both sugars on medium-high speed until light and fluffy. Mix in the eggs and vanilla until well combined.
  4. Stir the dry ingredients into the butter mixture, then stir in the toffee bits.
  5. Use a rubber spatula to transfer the batter to the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes. Allow the bars to cool for a few minutes, then cut into 24 pieces. Serve warm or let cool completely.

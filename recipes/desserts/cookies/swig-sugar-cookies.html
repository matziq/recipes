Swig Sugar Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Available ingredient facts

  • Cookies: 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup avocado oil (cup unit recovered from original)
  • 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon each baking soda, cream of tartar, and salt
  • 2 tablespoons sour cream
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 5 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • Granulated sugar for pressing
  • Frosting: 3/4 cup butter, softened
  • 2 tablespoons sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 6 cups powdered sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons cream or milk

Instructions (concise adaptation)

  1. Heat oven to 350 F (325 F convection). Line baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Beat butter, oil, both sugars, baking soda, cream of tartar, and salt until creamy, 1-2 minutes. Beat in sour cream, eggs, and vanilla for another 1-2 minutes.
  3. Mix in flour just until combined. Roll approximately 3-tablespoon portions into balls, spacing them several inches apart.
  4. Dip the greased base of a flat-bottomed glass into granulated sugar and press each ball to 1/4-1/2 inch thick, dipping again between cookies.
  5. The supplied version specifies 10-12 minutes until just set, without browned edges. The current original instead specifies 7-9 minutes until edges are set. This page preserves the supplied version rather than silently replacing its timing.
  6. Cool briefly on the sheets, then completely on a rack. Beat frosting butter, sour cream and vanilla until smooth; mix in powdered sugar and cream or milk to a spreadable consistency. Frost cooled cookies; the supplied version says to serve chilled.

Yield: 30 cookies.

Original identified: Mel Gunnell, Mel's Kitchen Cafe, The Best Swig Sugar Cookies. The cookie/frosting cup quantities, yield, and distinctive preparation sequence match the supplied document. The original explicitly gives 3/4 cup oil; only that missing unit was recovered. The supplied avocado-oil substitution is retained. Current original weight conversions, oil options, and baking time differ and were not substituted. Directions are newly summarized, not copied.

About this dish / Did you know?

The word cookie comes from the Dutch koekje, meaning a little cake. Rolled, dropped and bar cookies are different ways of shaping this broad baking family, rather than names for one fixed dough.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups oats
  • 1 cup coconut
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Bring sugar, milk, butter, cocoa, and salt to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Off heat, stir in oats, coconut, and vanilla. Spoon onto wax paper or spread in a pan; let cool.

About this dish / Did you know?

Despite their name, peanuts are legumes rather than true botanical nuts. Their pods develop underground after flowering, which is why groundnut is another common name for the same food.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (8 tbsp; 113 g) melted salted butter
  • 1 cup (120 g) graham cracker crumbs
  • 2 cups (240 g) confectioners' sugar
  • 1 cup plus 2 tbsp (280 g) creamy peanut butter, divided
  • 1 cup (180 g) semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Line an 8- or 9-inch square pan with parchment or foil.
  2. Mix butter, crumbs, and sugar; stir in 1 cup (250 g) peanut butter and press evenly into the pan.
  3. Melt chips with the remaining 2 tbsp peanut butter, stir smooth, and spread on the base.
  4. Refrigerate at least 2 hours until firm; rest at room temperature 10 minutes before cutting. Serve chilled.

Source limitation / note: Makes 20-24 squares. Keep tightly covered up to 1 week refrigerated or 3 months frozen; thaw refrigerated. Unsalted butter alternative: add 1/4 tsp salt. Use processed creamy peanut butter rather than oily natural peanut butter; 120 g digestive biscuits can replace graham crumbs.

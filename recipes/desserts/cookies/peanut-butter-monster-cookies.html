Peanut Butter Monster Cookies

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups mini peanut butter cups, halved
  • 2/3 cup peanut butter/chocolate chips
  • Reese's Pieces or mini Reese's Pieces, amount unspecified

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 325 F; line sheets with parchment. Sift flour, salt, and soda together.
  2. Beat butter, peanut butter, and sugars about 1 minute; beat in egg and vanilla about 1 minute.
  3. Mix in flour on low, then gently fold in cups and chips.
  4. Dip heaping tablespoon portions in candy pieces and place 3-4 inches apart.
  5. Bake about 15 minutes, until lightly golden with a few cracks; do not overbake.

Coconut Macaroons

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz flaked coconut
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 4 tbsp margarine or butter
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Mix coconut, flour, and salt; stir in condensed milk and vanilla.
  2. Drop rounded spoonfuls on a well-greased sheet. Bake at 375 F 12-13 minutes until some coconut is toasted; cool.
  3. Microwave chips with butter or margarine 1-2 minutes and stir smooth. Dip half of each cookie, then refrigerate on a plastic-wrap-covered sheet.

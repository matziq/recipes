Peanut Butter Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Creamy Peanut Butter
  • 1 8 oz Package of Cream Cheese
  • 1 16 oz Tub of Cool Whip
  • 1 cup Powdered Sugar
  • 1 Hershey Bar
  • Oreo Pie Crust (Crush 25 oreos, melt ½ cup butter, mix together and form into crust)

Instructions

  1. Mix first 4 ingredients in a mixer until smooth and heavy. Spoon mixture into pie crust. Shave Hershey Bar into sprinkles over the top of the pie. Freeze for 8 hours. Thaw for 30 minutes before serving. Serve with thigh bone.

About this dish / Did you know?

Ingredients

Ganache

  • Generous 2 cups (18 oz; 500 g) heavy cream
  • 21 oz (600 g) finely chopped bittersweet chocolate
  • Generous 1/4 cup (2 oz; 60 g) Grand Marnier or Stoli Razberi vodka, optional

Coating

  • 18 oz (500 g) tempered bittersweet chocolate
  • 18 oz (500 g) tempered white chocolate

Garnishes

  • 2 cups (8 oz; 230 g) sifted Dutch-process cocoa
  • 2 1/2 cups (9 oz; 250 g) toasted sweetened shredded coconut
  • About 2 cups (8 oz; 230 g) finely chopped toasted nuts

Instructions

  1. Heat cream until bubbles appear at the saucepan edge. Pour half over chopped chocolate, wait 30 seconds, and whisk smooth. Gradually incorporate the rest of the cream, then optional flavoring.
  2. Spread ganache on a plastic-wrap-lined sheet, cover, and let set at least 4 hours until pipeable.
  3. Pipe or spoon 1-inch mounds, spaced 1 inch apart. Let firm for a couple of hours, or refrigerate 15 minutes, then roll into balls.
  4. If too soft to dip, refrigerate 1-2 hours. Dip in the tempered coating of choice, drain, and apply garnish before chocolate sets. Hand-coated truffles need a second coating; let set about 5 minutes.
  5. For coconut, toast at 400 F about 3 minutes, stir, and toast about 3 more minutes to golden. Toast nuts at 300 F about 30 minutes; cool before use.

Natural vanilla comes from the cured seed pods of a tropical climbing orchid. Curing develops the familiar aroma, so the dark, fragrant vanilla bean used in cooking is not simply a freshly picked pod.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pecan halves
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2/3 cup brown sugar
  • 14 oz sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Microwave pecans in a glass pie plate for 5 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes.
  2. In an 8-cup microwave-safe measure, melt butter for 1 minute. Stir in sugar and milk; microwave 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes. The source warns of boil-over.
  3. Beat about 3 minutes, stir in pecans and vanilla, and quickly spoon onto wax paper to cool.

Source limitation / note: Microwave power is unspecified; use caution with hot syrup and boil-over.

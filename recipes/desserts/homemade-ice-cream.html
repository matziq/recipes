Homemade Ice Cream

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • 3 cups sugar
  • 1 quart ½ & ½
  • 3 tablespoons of flavoring
  • Skim milk

Instructions

  1. Fluff eggs and sugar together very well
  2. Add ½ & ½ and flavoring and mix well
  3. Pour into ice cream freezer
  4. Top off freezer with skim milk to fill line

CINNAMON FLAVORING

  1. Melt 1 bag of red hots with ¼ cup water

About this dish / Did you know?

Ice cream usually combines dairy, sugar and flavoring, while frozen custard also uses eggs. Those are related frozen-dessert traditions, not interchangeable formulas; the ingredients and method for this particular version remain the guide.

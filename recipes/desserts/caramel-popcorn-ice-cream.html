Caramel Popcorn Ice Cream

Prep: about 30 minutes, plus chilling and 24 hours of freezing

Ingredients

  • 50 g caramel popcorn, divided
  • 300 ml full-fat cream
  • 30 g sugar, optional and adjustable to taste
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 100 ml whole milk

Directions

  • Keep about 30 g of the popcorn aside for the mix-in. Warm the cream with the remaining popcorn until it is just beginning to simmer, then take it off the heat and steep for 15 to 20 minutes.
  • Pour the infused cream through a fine strainer. Press the softened popcorn gently to recover the flavored cream, then discard those pieces.
  • While the cream is still warm, whisk in the optional sugar until dissolved. Add the milk and vanilla. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  • Transfer the cold base to a Ninja Creami or Ninja Swirl container. Freeze it level for 24 hours.
  • Process the frozen base using the Ice Cream program.
  • Coarsely crush the reserved popcorn. Make a hole through the center of the ice cream, add the pieces, and run the Mix-In program.
  • Serve right away for a soft texture, or freeze for another 20 to 30 minutes for firmer scoops.

Tip: Crisp popcorn gives the best contrast. Taste the base before adding sugar because caramel popcorn may already make it sweet enough.

Ferrero Rocher Ice Cream

Prep: about 30 minutes, plus chilling and 24 hours of freezing

Ingredients

  • 100 g Ferrero Rocher chocolates, about 7 to 8 pieces
  • 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 200 g oat milk
  • 50 g heavy cream
  • 75 g powdered sugar
  • 2 egg yolks

Directions

  • Blend the chocolates into as smooth a paste as possible, then set it aside.
  • Heat the oat milk and cream in a saucepan over medium heat until they are just starting to simmer.
  • In a bowl, whisk the yolks and sugar until pale and slightly foamy. Add the cocoa and whisk until no lumps remain.
  • Whisk a small splash of hot milk into the yolks to temper them, then gradually add the rest. Return everything to the saucepan.
  • Cook over low heat while stirring constantly until the custard coats a spatula or reaches 84°C (183°F).
  • Remove from the heat and stir in the chocolate-hazelnut paste. Cool fully, then refrigerate until cold.
  • Pour into a Ninja Creami or Ninja Swirl container and freeze level for 24 hours.
  • Run the Ice Cream program, or Soft Serve on a Ninja Swirl. If the result looks powdery or grainy, use Re-Spin.

Tip: Thoroughly blending the chocolates before making the custard produces the smoothest finished texture.

Sweet Coconut Tapioca Soup with Bananas (Che Chuoi)

This dessert soup can be eaten hot, at room temperature, or chilled. The cooler the soup gets, the thicker it becomes.

Ingredients

  • 4  cups water
  • 2  (14-ounce) cans light coconut milk
  • 1  cup sugar
  • 1/2  cup uncooked granulated tapioca
  • 1  teaspoon salt
  • 4  large ripe bananas, quartered lengthwise and cut into 12/-inch pieces (about 3 cups)
  • 2  teaspoons sesame seeds, toasted

Instructions

  1. Bring water and coconut milk to a boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in sugar, tapioca, and salt. Reduce heat to medium-low; cook 30 minutes, stirring frequently. Stir in bananas. Remove from heat, cover, and let stand 15 minutes. Serve war, or chill 3 hours. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, if desired.

Yield: 8 servings

About this dish / Did you know?

A popcorn kernel traps moisture inside a strong hull. Heating turns that moisture into steam until the hull gives way and the starchy interior expands into the familiar fluffy snack; this is a property of popping corn, not every kind of dried corn.

Instructions

  1. Microwave until caramel boils, then stir in ½ teaspoon of soda (use a much bigger bowl than you anticipate b/c the caramel grows with the addition of the soda!). Pour caramel over popcorn and stir. Sprinkle lightly with sea salt, stir thoroughly and enjoy!

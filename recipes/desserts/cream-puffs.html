Cream Puffs

Ingredients

  • 1 c water
  • ½ c butter
  • 4 eggs
  • ½ t salt
  • 1 c flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Heat butter and water to boiling point. Add flour and salt all at once; stir vigorously until mixture leaves sides of pan and clings to spoon. Remove from heat and cool slightly. Add unbeaten eggs, 1 at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition. Drop by spoonfuls on greased sheet 1 ½ inches apart. Shape into rounds about 2 inches in diameter. Bake for 40 minutes for large puffs, 30 minutes for small.
  3. When ready to use, make a cut in top of each with a sharp knife and fill.

Mousse Filling

  • 1 large or 2 small boxes of instant pudding
  • 1 pint heavy whipping cream
  • 2 t clear vanilla (regular vanilla will work, but will turn it slightly brownish)
  • ¼ c sugar (or to taste)
  • 3 oz chocolate chips
  • 2 T evaporated milk
  • Powdered sugar
  1. prepare a large box of instant pudding (or if you want two flavors, mix two small boxes) as directed and chill until set. While pudding is setting, make some whipped cream (add vanilla and sugar once it has been whipped into peaks).
  2. Mix into pudding to make a light, fluffy mousse.
  3. Fill cream puffs with mousse, top with remaining whipped cream, drizzle with melted chocolate (melt chocolate chips with a little evaporated milk), and sprinkle with a little powdered sugar. ENJOY!

Cajun Pork Chops

Main dishes · Pork

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp rubbed sage
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne, to taste
  • 1/2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tbsp oil
  • 4 boneless center-cut pork chops, thickness printed as 1/2 without a unit
  • Salt, mentioned in method; amount unspecified

Instructions

  1. Combine paprika, salt, sage, pepper, garlic powder, and cayenne; coat both sides of the chops.
  2. Heat butter and oil in a skillet on high. Add chops, reduce to medium, and cook 8-9 minutes, turning halfway.
  3. The source also allows grilling until done.

Source limitation / note: Cumin is listed but omitted from the source method, and salt is used without a listed amount. Thickness unit is missing. Times are estimates, not proof of safe doneness.

About this dish / Did you know?

Onions, garlic, leeks and chives belong to the same plant genus, Allium. Their related aromas explain why these plants appear so often as seasonings, despite the different shapes and textures of their bulbs and leaves.

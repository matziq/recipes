Walnut Crusted Pork Chops With Wild Rice

Main dishes · Pork

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Pork

  • 4 trimmed 8-oz bone-in center-cut pork chops
  • 1 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp dry sherry
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp dried sage
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/3 cup finely ground walnuts
  • 1 bacon slice
  • 1/4 cup chicken broth

Rice

  • Cooking spray
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup diced carrot
  • 2 tsp diced seeded jalapeno, optional
  • 1 minced garlic clove
  • 1 1/2 cups finely chopped spinach
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup chopped peeled Granny Smith apple
  • 2 cups cooked wild rice
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley

Instructions

  1. Drizzle Worcestershire over chops. Mix salt, Dijon, sherry, honey, sage, and pepper; reserve 1 tsp of that mixture. Add walnuts to the remainder, coat chops, and refrigerate 30 minutes.
  2. Cook bacon crisp, remove and crumble. Brown chops in drippings 1 1/2 minutes per side. Add 1/4 cup broth, cover, reduce heat, and cook about 6 minutes; remove chops.
  3. In a sprayed saucepan over medium heat, cook onion, carrot, optional jalapeno, and garlic covered for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the reserved seasoning, spinach, mushrooms, and apple; cover 5 minutes until carrot is tender. Stir in bacon.
  5. Serve 1 cup rice mixture with each chop, pan drippings, and 1 tbsp parsley.

About this dish / Did you know?

Rice is the edible grain of a grass, and it is used far beyond a plain side dish. It can also be ground into flour or made into noodles and drinks, so rice-based recipes can have very different textures and roles at the table.

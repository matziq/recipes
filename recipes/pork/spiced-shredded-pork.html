Spiced Shredded Pork

Main dishes · Pork

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pork tenderloin
  • 2 (14 1/2-ounce) cans low-sodium chicken stock (about 4 cups)
  • 2 small onions, finely chopped (about 1/2 cup)
  • 1/2 fresh jalapeno with seeds, finely chopped
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro (2 1/2-3 cups), plus garnish
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Coarse salt and freshly ground pepper
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the broiler. Combine pork, stock, half the onion, jalapeno, cilantro, cumin, bay leaf, and 1/2 teaspoon salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then simmer, stirring occasionally.
  2. The source specifies 10-15 minutes and 140 F before broiling. Transfer to a rimmed baking sheet and shred into 2-inch pieces.
  3. Add the remaining onion, butter, and oil, and season with salt and pepper. Broil 4-7 minutes until golden.

Adapted from the supplied sheet. Food safety: the source's 140 F interim temperature is not the final safe temperature. Whole-cut pork must reach 145 F and rest at least 3 minutes before serving.

About this dish / Did you know?

Onions, garlic, leeks and chives belong to the same plant genus, Allium. Their related aromas explain why these plants appear so often as seasonings, despite the different shapes and textures of their bulbs and leaves.

Cafe Rio Pork

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

Pork

  • 3 lb pork roast
  • 16 oz salsa
  • 1 can Dr. Pepper, size unspecified
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • Water to half-fill crockpot

Dressing

  • 1 envelope buttermilk ranch dressing
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro
  • 2 tomatillos
  • 2 serrano peppers, seeded

Rice

  • 1 cup rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 cubes chicken bouillon
  • 1/2 tsp dried onion
  • 1/4 bunch cilantro
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 lime

Assembly

  • Black or pinto beans, amount unspecified
  • Mozzarella cheese, amount unspecified
  • Cooked tortillas, amount unspecified
  • Corn, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook pork on low with water halfway up the crockpot for 4 hours. Drain and cut into 3-4 pieces.
  2. Combine salsa, Dr. Pepper, and sugar; add to pork and cook on low 4 more hours. Shred about 10 minutes before serving.
  3. Blend dressing ingredients; refrigerate or let thicken.
  4. Cook rice with its listed ingredients except lime; then mix in juice of half the lime.
  5. Fill cooked tortillas with beans, cheese, rice, pork, and optional corn. Add 1/4 cup dressing inside and another 1/4 cup on top after folding.

Source limitation / note: Source times are estimates: verify safe pork doneness. Can size, ranch-packet size, tortilla count, and unused half-lime purpose are unspecified.

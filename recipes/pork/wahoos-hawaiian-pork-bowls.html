Wahoo’s Hawaiian Pork Bowls

For the Pork

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs. pork roast
  • 1 teaspoon Ono’s Hawaiian Seasoning
  • ½ onion
  • 2 tablespoons Liquid Smoke

Instructions

  1. Take a sharp knife and stab meat every inch over the roast. Rub Ono’s into pork well, then pour liquid smoke over and rub roast around in the sauce so it can absorb it. Top with sliced onion rings. Cook in 225 degree over for approximately 5 hours, or in a crockpot on high for 8-10 hours. About 10 minutes before serving, shred pork and let sauce absorb.

Dressing

  • Herdez Guacamole Salsa
  • Ranch dressing
  1. Use your favorite ranch dressing, or add a portion of the dried ranch dressing packet to the salsa. Mix according to taste. Some people like it heavy on the ranch, some like it heavy on the salsa.

Rice

  • 1 cup brown or white rice (to your taste)
  • 1 ½ cups water for white, 2 cups water for brown rice
  • 1 ½ teaspoons chicken bouillon for white, 2 for brown
  • 1 tsp. dried onion or a dash of onion salt
  • 1 Tb. Oil
  • 1 lime
  1. 1) Cook rice in above mixture, except lime.
  2. 2) Add juice of ½ lime and mix well.

Black Beans

  • 1 or 2 cans black beans, rinsed and seasoned

(optional)

  • ½ onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
  • 1 jalapeno
  • ½ red bell pepper
  1. Rinse beans and seasoned with cumin, Mexican oregano, garlic salt, onion salt, cayenne pepper, and a little bit of salsa. If you are feeling fancy, cut up onions, peppers, and carrots. Cook them until soft in olive oil, add rinsed and seasoned beans and a little chicken broth. Let the broth cook off. Mash about half of the beans and make a nice little stew! If you aren’t feeling fancy, you can just use the rinsed and seasoned beans.

Toppings (feel free to customize to your taste)

  • Salsa
  • Chopped fresh tomatoes
  • Avocado
  • Sour Cream
  • Cilantro
  • Napa Cabbage shredded
  • Queso fresco
  1. Layer rice, pork, beans, and whatever toppings you love, drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

Source note: Source cooking times are estimates; verify pork is cooked safely rather than relying on time alone.

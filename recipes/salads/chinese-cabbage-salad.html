Chinese Cabbage Salad
A delicious salad with a variety of tastes and textures.

Prep Time: approx. 15 Minutes. Cook Time: approx. 10 Minutes.
Ready in: approx. 25 Minutes. Makes 12 servings.
   

Salad:

1 (16 ounce) package shredded coleslaw mix

or 1 head cabbage, sliced thin
4-6 green onions, chopped

1 can chicken or 2 cooked chicken breasts, cut up

Topping:

1 (3 ounce) package Ramen noodles, broken
10 ounces cashew pieces

4 tsp. sesame seeds

Dressing

1/4 cup white sugar
1/2-1 cup olive oil
2 Tbsp. vinegar
1 tablespoon soy sauce

2 tsp. salt

1 tsp. pepper

2 flavor packets from Ramen Noodles

Directions
1 In a preheated 350 degree F oven, toast the Ramen noodles, nuts and sesame seeds until golden brown.
2 In a large bowl, combine the coleslaw, green onions and chicken
3 To prepare the dressing, whisk together the sugar, oil, vinegar and soy sauce, salt, pepper and Ramen flavor packets.

4 Keep all 3 steps separate until just before serving - toss and enjoy!

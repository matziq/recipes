Greek Couscous Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 box couscous
  • 1/2 t salt
  • 1/4 t black pepper
  • 3 T olive oil
  • 2 T lemon juice
  • 1 12 oz. bag of frozen broccoli - heated
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil, cut into strips
  • 3/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
  • 1/2 cup Artichoke hearts
  • 1/2 cup Sun dried tomatoes

Instructions

  1. In a 2-quart bowl prepare couscous according to “microwave directions” on couscous box. Add remaining ingredients to prepared couscous.

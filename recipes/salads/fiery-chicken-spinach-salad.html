Fiery Chicken Spinach Salad

Ingredients

  • 6 frozen breaded spicy chicken breast strips
  • 1 package (6 ounces) fresh baby spinach
  • 1 medium tomato cut into 12 wedges
  • ½ cup chopped green pepper
  • ½ cup fresh baby carrots
  • 1 can (18 ounces) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (11 ounces) Mexicorn, drained
  • 3 tablespoons salsa
  • 3 tablespoons barbecue sauce
  • 3 tablespoons prepared ranch dressing
  • 2 tablespoons shredded Mexican blend cheese

Instructions

  1. Heat chicken strips in microwave according to package directions. Meanwhile, arrange the spinach on individual plates; top with tomato, green pepper, carrots, beans and corn. In a small bowl combine the salsa, barbecue sauce and ranch dressing. Place chicken over salads. Drizzle with dressing; sprinkle with cheese.

Prep/Total: 10 min

Yield: 6 servings

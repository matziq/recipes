Grandma Barbie's Green Glow Crunch

A bright, no-cook chopped salad of cucumber, avocado, cilantro and green onion, finished with crumbled feta, chili-lime pistachios and a squeeze of fresh lime. Crunchy, creamy and on the table in ten minutes.

Yield: 2 servings • Prep/Total: 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 English cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro
  • 1-2 green onions
  • 1/4 cup pistachios (chili lime or jalapeno lime are my favorite!)
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese (generously rounded)
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • Salt, to taste
  • Chili lime seasoning, to taste (from Trader Joe's or Tajin)
  • Olive oil, for drizzling (optional)

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the cucumber, cilantro and green onions. Dice the avocado and gently mix it in.
  2. Sprinkle with the crumbled feta and pistachios.
  3. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top.
  4. Season with salt and chili lime to taste.
  5. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil. Enjoy!

A family recipe from Grandma Barbie.

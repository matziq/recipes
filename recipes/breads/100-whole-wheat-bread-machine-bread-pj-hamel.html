100% Whole Wheat Bread Machine Bread (PJ Hamel)

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

This complete substitute is separate from the incomplete original. Ingredient quantities and cooking facts follow the cited published recipe; directions are independently worded. No source photographs or protected article text are reproduced. This site has not test-cooked it.

Yield: 1 loaf; the same dough fits an 8 1/2 by 4 1/2-inch oven loaf pan

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups (283 grams) lukewarm water
  • 2 tablespoons (25 grams) olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cup honey (85 grams) or maple syrup (78 grams)
  • 3 1/2 cups (397 grams) golden whole wheat flour
  • 1/4 cup (35 grams) sunflower, sesame, or flax seeds, optional
  • 1 tablespoon vital wheat gluten, optional for higher rise
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons (9 grams) table salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast

Instructions

  1. Weigh flour or spoon it lightly into a cup and level. Check your bread machine's flour/loaf capacity before loading this batch.
  2. Place ingredients in the bread pan in the listed order. Select basic white or, if provided, the whole-wheat program and start.
  3. After the machine's full cycle finishes, remove the hot pan with oven mitts and turn the loaf onto a rack. Let cool before wrapping or slicing.

The publisher developed this formula for a Zojirushi machine but does not give a universal loaf-weight setting or cycle duration. Do not guess a rapid mode or use a machine too small for this flour quantity.

Complete oven alternative with the same ingredients: mix and knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and slightly tacky; cover and rise 1-1 1/2 hours until doubled. Deflate, shape in a greased 8 1/2 by 4 1/2-inch pan, and rise another 1-1 1/2 hours until doubled. Bake at 375 F about 35 minutes, until the center is 190 F, then remove from the pan and cool.

This differs materially from the existing two-loaf whole-grain wheat bread with agave and molasses; neither recipe is replaced.

Recipe source: PJ Hamel, King Arthur Baking, 100% Whole Wheat Bread for the Bread Machine. Reviewed November 7, 2026.

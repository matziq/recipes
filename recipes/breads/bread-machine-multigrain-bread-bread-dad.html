Bread Machine Multigrain Bread (Bread Dad)

Yield: 1 loaf, about 12 slices; choose ONE loaf-size group

Ingredients

1.5-pound loaf

  • 1 1/8 cups (266 milliliters) warm milk
  • 3 tablespoons (43 grams) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 1/4 cups (270 grams) bread flour
  • 3/4 cup (120 grams) dry finely chopped multigrain hot cereal
  • 1/4 cup (54 grams) packed light brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon (6 grams) salt
  • 1 teaspoon (3.6 grams) bread machine yeast

2-pound loaf; use INSTEAD of the 1.5-pound group

  • 1 1/2 cups (355 milliliters) warm milk
  • 4 tablespoons (57 grams) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cups (360 grams) bread flour
  • 1 cup (160 grams) dry finely chopped multigrain hot cereal
  • 1/3 cup (72 grams) packed light brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons (9 grams) salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons (5.4 grams) bread machine yeast

Instructions

  1. Verify machine capacity and choose one loaf-size group. The publisher recommends trying the 1.5-pound batch first, even in some 2-pound machines. Neither group fits a 1-pound-only machine.
  2. Use dry cereal made of small chopped grains and seeds, not large breakfast flakes. Do not soak it beforehand.
  3. With the machine unplugged, remove its pan. Add milk and the remaining ingredients, with yeast last and separated from liquid and salt until mixing begins.
  4. Install the pan, close the lid, plug in, and select the matching 1.5- or 2-pound loaf, light crust, and basic/white cycle. Start the complete cycle.
  5. When baking finishes, unplug and remove the hot pan using oven mitts. Turn the loaf onto a rack and cool 1-2 hours before slicing.

The two size groups are alternatives, never a combined batch. The publisher lists an approximately 3-hour cycle; actual duration follows the machine.

For a less sweet loaf, the source permits 1 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar in the 1.5-pound group or 2 tablespoons in the 2-pound group. It also permits an equal amount of olive oil instead of butter.

This coherent multigrain cereal formula does not reconstruct every protected manual-table variant or establish its multigrain/rapid program.

Recipe source: Bread Dad, Bread Machine Multigrain Bread. Reviewed November 7, 2026.

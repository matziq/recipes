Butter Naan

Soft skillet-cooked naan finished with melted butter and optional cilantro.

Yield: 8 naan • Prep: 15 min • Cook: 2 min

Ingredients

  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon instant dry yeast
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 3/4 cup plain yogurt (or buttermilk)
  • 1 cup warm water (120 to 130 degrees F)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon minced cilantro (optional)
  • Neutral oil, for coating the dough

Instructions

  1. In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle, combine the flour, yeast, salt, baking soda, and baking powder on low speed.
  2. Add the yogurt and warm water. Mix on low until a shaggy dough forms, then switch to the dough hook and knead on medium-low for 4 minutes. The dough should remain sticky.
  3. Generously flour the work surface and turn out the dough. Knead it once or twice into a ball, divide it into 8 equal portions, and shape each portion into a ball. Lightly oil the balls on a parchment-lined surface, cover, and let rise for 1 hour.
  4. On a floured surface, roll each ball to about 1/8 inch thick. Gently stretch it into a thin oval and let the shaped pieces rest in a single layer for 10 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high for 5 minutes, until a drop of water beads and evaporates immediately.
  6. Place one naan in the hot skillet, cover, and cook for 30 to 60 seconds, until bubbles expand across the surface.
  7. Flip and cook for about 30 seconds more, until the second side no longer looks raw and develops charred spots.
  8. Transfer cooked naan to a plate and keep it covered with a towel while cooking the remaining pieces.
  9. Stir the optional cilantro into the melted butter and brush it over each naan while warm.

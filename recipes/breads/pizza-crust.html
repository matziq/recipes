Pizza Crust

1 c quite warm water

1 T yeast

1 t sugar

2 T oil

2 ½ c flour

1 t salt

Preheat oven to 425°F.

Dissolve yeast in warm water then add sugar. Let sit until mixture foams up. Add oil and flour and mix until moistened and then add salt and mix well.

Cover and let rest for 5 minutes.

Press onto greased pizza pan with oiled fingers and bake at 425°F until crust is a light golden brown, about 13 minutes.

Add desired sauce and toppings and return pizza to oven until ingredients are melted, warmed and/or cooked.

Makes 1 18-inch crust or 2 12-inch crusts.

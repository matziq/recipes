Exquisite Pumpkin Bread

3 c canned pumpkin

1 1/2 c vegetable oil

4 c white sugar

6 eggs

4 3/4 c all-purpose flour

1 1/2 t baking powder

1 1/2 t baking soda

1 1/2 t salt

1 1/2 t ground cinnamon

1 1/2 t ground nutmeg

1 1/2 t ground cloves

  • Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  • Grease and flour 6 mini-loaf pans
  • In a large bowl, mix together the pumpkin, oil, sugar and eggs. Combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves; stir into the pumpkin mixture until well blended. Divide the batter evenly between the pans.
  • Bake in preheated oven for 35+ minutes. The top of the loaf should spring back when lightly pressed.
  • (You can also make muffins or 3 large loaves with this recipe; increase baking time 45 min-1 hour for large loaves.)

Makes 8 servings

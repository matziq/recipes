Buttermilk Biscuits (Holly Nilsson)

Ready

Yield: 10 biscuits

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup cold butter
  • 1 cup buttermilk, adjusted by about 2 tablespoons if needed

Instructions

  1. Set the oven to 450 F. Stir the flour, baking powder, sugar, baking soda, and salt together.
  2. Cut the cold butter into the dry mixture with a pastry cutter or forks, leaving pea-size pieces.
  3. Stir in buttermilk gradually until the dough holds together without overmixing; use a little more or less only if necessary.
  4. On a lightly floured surface, knead briefly, pat into a square, and fold the dough over itself 5-6 times.
  5. Pat to 1-inch thickness. Cut straight down with a sharp biscuit cutter without twisting; gather and cut the scraps.
  6. Bake on an ungreased baking sheet for 10-12 minutes, until golden.

The publisher's summary lists 20 minutes cook time, but its actual baking instruction says 10-12 minutes; the latter is preserved here. Keep the butter cold and handle the dough gently.

Recipe source: Holly Nilsson, Spend With Pennies, Homemade Buttermilk Biscuits. Reviewed November 7, 2026.

Banana-Oatmeal Loaf

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 3 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 ½ cup regular oats
  • 2 cups mashed ripe banana (about 4 large)
  • 2/3 cup low-fat buttermilk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup walnuts
  • Cooking Spray

Instructions

  1. Preheat over to 35oo
  2. Lightly spoon flour into dry measuring cups; level with a knife. Combine flour and the next 4 ingredients (through salt) in a large bowl, stirring well with a whisk. Stir in oats
  3. Combine mashed banana, buttermilk, vegetable oil, vanilla extract, and eggs; add to flour mixture and nuts. Stir just until moist
  4. Spoon batter into 5 mini-loaves (or 2 regular loaves) coated with cooking spray. Bake at 35oo for 45 minutes for mini-loaves or 55 minutes for large loaves – or until edges begin to pull away from pan and a wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool 15 minutes in pan on a wire rack; remove from pan. Cool completely on wire rack.

Makes 5 mini-loaves or 2 regular loaves

About this dish / Did you know?

Baking powder and baking soda can raise a batter by releasing carbon dioxide rather than waiting for yeast fermentation. They work differently and are not automatic one-for-one substitutes, so the leavener specified in this recipe matters.

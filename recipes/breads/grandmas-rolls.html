Grandma’s Famous Rolls

Ingredients

  • 3 T. Yeast
  • 3 C. Plenty-warm water
  • ¾ C. Sugar
  • ¾ C. Butter
  • 3 t. Salt
  • 4 Beaten eggs
  • 6-8 C. Flour

Instructions

  1. Mix all together. Put water in bowl. Sprinkle yeast on top, then add sugar, butter and let butter melt in water. Add remaining ingredients and mix together.
  2. Let rise to top of bowl. Punch dough down and let rise a second time. Put lid on bowl and put in fridge for several hours.
  3. Take dough out of bowl. Pat or roll dough out. Cut into rectangles, dip ½ the rectangle in melted butter and fold in half. Place rolls close together on “pammed” pan. Let them rise for 20 minutes (or until they look like what you want them to look like as a finished product).
  4. OVEN: 400o
  5. BAKE: 10 minutes

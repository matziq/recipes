During proofing, yeast fermentation releases carbon dioxide that helps dough rise. The bubbles explain why rising time is part of many bread methods, rather than merely a pause between mixing and baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 to 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet Fleischmann's Pizza Crust Yeast
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2/3 cup very warm water (120-130 F)
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • Pizza sauce, toppings, and cheese

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425 F. Mix 1 cup flour with the undissolved yeast, sugar, and salt.
  2. Stir in water and oil for about 1 minute. Add enough remaining flour to make a soft, slightly sticky dough.
  3. Knead on a floured surface for about 4 minutes, adding flour as needed, until smooth and elastic.
  4. Pat into a greased pizza pan or roll into a 12-inch circle and transfer to a greased pan. Add sauce, toppings, and cheese.
  5. Bake on the bottom oven rack for 12-15 minutes, until the crust browns and cheese bubbles.

Cranberry Nut Bread

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole-wheat flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/3 cup applesauce
  • 3 tbsp buttermilk
  • 1 1/2 cups fresh cranberries
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 F and lightly spray a loaf pan.
  2. Combine flours, sugar, cinnamon, salt, and soda. Beat egg whites on high for 1 minute, then beat in applesauce and buttermilk. Gradually beat in the flour mixture just until combined; stir in cranberries and walnuts.
  3. Transfer to the prepared pan. Bake 45 minutes, or until edges pull away and a toothpick comes out clean. Slice and serve.

Easy Bread Machine Bread (PJ Hamel)

Yield: 1 loaf; choose ONE ingredient group for the machine's capacity

Ingredients

Large 1 1/2-2-pound machine ONLY

  • 1 cup (227 grams) lukewarm water
  • 1/3 cup (74 grams) lukewarm milk
  • 3 tablespoons (43 grams) butter
  • 3 3/4 cups (450 grams) unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoons (35 grams) granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons (9 grams) table salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons active dry yeast or instant yeast

Small 1-pound machine ONLY; use INSTEAD of the large group

  • 2/3 cup (152 grams) lukewarm water
  • 1/4 cup (57 grams) lukewarm milk
  • 2 tablespoons (28 grams) butter
  • 2 1/2 cups (300 grams) unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 5 teaspoons granulated sugar
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons (8 grams) table salt
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast or instant yeast

Instructions

  1. Check the machine's capacity and select just one of the two ingredient groups. Weigh flour, or spoon it into a cup and level without packing.
  2. Load that group's ingredients in the order specified by your machine manufacturer.
  3. Select the standard basic-white bread cycle and start. Let the machine complete its own kneading, rising, and baking cycle.
  4. Use oven mitts to remove the hot pan. After about 5 minutes, release the loaf onto a cooling rack.
  5. Wrap after cooling; keep at room temperature for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.

The two ingredient groups are alternatives, not one combined batch. Cycle duration depends on the machine; the published recipe does not establish a rapid-cycle equivalent.

Recipe source: PJ Hamel, King Arthur Baking, Easy Bread Machine Bread. Reviewed November 7, 2026.

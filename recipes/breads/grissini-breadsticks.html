Grissini Breadsticks

Ready
Cook mode is on: larger text and screen wake lock when supported.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water (105-115 F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 package)
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup semolina flour
  • 2 teaspoons honey
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • Seasoning: 1 tablespoon each dried oregano, dried basil, dried parsley, red pepper flakes, and granulated garlic; 1 1/2 teaspoons dried rosemary; 1 teaspoon each fennel seeds and kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Dissolve active dry yeast in the warm water and wait 5-10 minutes for foam. Stir in oil and honey. For instant yeast, mix it with the flour instead.
  2. Combine both flours and salt, then add to the liquid. Knead 10 minutes until smooth; hand-kneaded dough may need 2 tablespoons extra flour. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. Stretch into a 14-by-4-inch rectangle. Brush with olive oil, cover the top and sides with plastic wrap, and proof at room temperature for 1 hour.
  4. Heat the oven to 450 F. Uncover and oil the dough again. Divide into quarters and keep unused portions covered.
  5. Pulse the seasoning ingredients in a small food processor. Press 2 teaspoons of seasoning onto each dough quarter, reaching the edges. Cut each quarter into 6 strips.
  6. Stretch each strip to 14-16 inches, twist, and arrange on parchment-lined baking sheets.
  7. Bake one sheet at a time for 10-12 minutes, turning the sticks after 6 minutes, until lightly browned.

Other toppings named in the source: coarse salt and pepper, sesame, Parmesan, poppy seeds, sun-dried tomato, or fennel and salt.

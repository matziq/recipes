Famous Stapelman Family Pizza

makes one 15-inch pizza or three personal sized pizzas

2/3 cup very warm water (1200 to 1300 F)

1 T yeast

1 ½ tsp sugar

1 ¾ - 2 cups flour

¾ tsp salt

3 T olive oil

Preheat and pizza stone t0 5250 F

Combine water, sugar and yeast and mix well. Cover with a cloth and leave until foamy

Combine flour, oil, and salt in a stand mixer (start with 1 ¾ c and add the additional ¼ c if dough is too sticky to work with after adding yeast mixture. Dough should come together and clean the bowl.)

Add yeast mixture to flour mixture and let stand mixer knead dough for 5 minutes

Roll to desired size, put on hot pizza stone and cook for 3 minutes or until golden brown

Top pizza with desired toppings and cook again for 4 minutes or until cheese is melted

Not-Yo-Mama’s pizza: marinara sauce, pepperoni, black olives, banana pepper rings, mushrooms, artichoke hearts, mozzarella cheese

Wise Guy: whipped cream cheese (spread on crust), fresh spinach, artichoke hearts, diced chicken (cooked in lemon juice, garlic salt, onion salt, & cayenne pepper), tangy marinara, mozzarella cheese, After Baking – diced tomatoes, fresh basil, balsamic vinaigrette reduction sauce

Cheeseburger: fry sauce (mayo, ketchup, salt), ground hamburger cooked with onions, pickles, tomatoes, top with American cheese, ketchup, and mustard, After Baking – lettuce

Chicken Alfredo: alfredo sauce, cooked chicken, crumbled bacon, mozzarella cheese

Buffalo Wing: ranch (or blue cheese) dressing mixed with buffalo sauce, shredded chicken, banana peppers, mozzarella cheese After Baking – top with a swirl of buffalo sauce

Chicken Caesar Cashew: Caesar dressing,, spinach, diced chicken (cooked in lemon juice, garlic salt, onion salt, & cayenne pepper), cashew pieces, mozzarella cheese

The Fungi: alfredo sauce, three types of mushrooms, caramelized onions, mozzarella cheese

BBQ Chicken: BBQ sauce, shredded chicken, purple onion rings, mozzarella cheese, drizzle with extra BBQ sauce

Margherita Pizza: pizza sauce, fresh mozzarella sliced or pearls (cut in ½), sliced roma tomatoes, After Baking top with fresh Basil.

Papa’s Pizza Supreme: Marinara sauce, pepperoni, Italian sausage, ham or Canadian bacon, bacon, mushrooms, olives, peppers, onions

Idaho: sour cream, mashed potatoes, crumbled bacon, cheddar cheese

Ranch Pepperoni: ranch dressing, pepperoni, mozzarella cheese

Jalapeno Popper: whipped cream cheese, quartered jalapenos (with seeds & ribs removed), crumbled bacon

Seafood: cook 3 T butter & 2 T milk until bubbling, add ¼ c chicken broth and ¼ c Monterey Jack or Swiss cheese to make a cheese sauce, add shrimp, crab, and scallops sautéed in butter, top with mozzarella and Swiss cheese

Taco: sour cream and salsa mixed, cooked hamburger seasoned with taco mix, black beans, cheddar cheese After Baking top with tomatoes and lettuce

Fajita: sour cream, fajita seasoned chicken or beef, roasted red peppers, roasted onions, mozzarella cheese After Baking top with avocado

Das German: sour cream mixed with stone ground mustard, Polka Kielbasa sausage, sauerkraut

Philly Cheesesteak: cheese whiz, roasted or grilled red peppers and onions, steak sliced paper thin, marinated & cooked (marinate in ½ T baking soda, 1 ½ T vinegar, 2 T sugar, 1 T garlic salt, ½ T onion powder, 2 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp salt, 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce)

Rueben: fry or Russian sauce, sliced thin corned beef, sauerkraut (squeeze dry), top with Swiss cheese

Southwestern Chicken: Sour cream mixed with salsa, cooked chicken, black beans, Pepper-Jack cheese

No Meat Treat: white sauce, artichoke hearts, grape tomatoes, chickpeas, sundried tomatoes, black olives, green olives (or kalamata), roasted red peppers, mushrooms, fresh spinach, salt & pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, mozzarella

Surf ‘n Turf: parmesan sauce (2 T butter, 2 T olive oil, 2 T minced garlic, 1 lg shallot, ¼ c chardonnay wine, ¼ c heavy cream, ¼ c fresh parmesan cheese, salt & pepper to taste. Melt 2 tablespoons butter with 2 tablespoons of olive oil in the skillet over medium heat until the foam subsides from the butter. Cook and stir the shallot and garlic until tender, about 2 minutes. Pour in the wine and cook and stir until the wine reduces by half, about 3 minutes. Stir in the cream, parmesan,1 tablespoon of butter, and a pinch of salt and pepper to taste. Cook and stir until the sauce is thickened and velvety) steak, marinated shrimp (Whisk 1 tablespoon each of olive oil, butter, onion, wine, 1 teaspoon each of Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice, parsley, Old Bay seasoning, garlic, and black pepper to taste - together in a bowl; add shrimp. Toss to coat evenly. Cover bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for flavors to blend, at least 15 minutes), mozzarella & parmesan cheese

Samoa: chunks of pecan sandies, chocolate chips, Mrs. Richardson’s Butterscotch Caramel sauce, shredded coconut. Bake until chocolate is melted and coconut is toasted.

Coco Berry Revelry: Nutella spread, fresh raspberries, panko crumble (panko crumbs, brown sugar butter mixed)

Oreo: chocolate pudding, crushed oreos

Carmel Apple Delight: base with a small amount of salted caramel sauce, dice apple and toss with 2 T flour, 2 T brown sugar, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp nutmeg and cook until soft, top with streusel (½ c flour, ½ c cold butter, ½ c oatmeal, 1/3 c brown sugar mixed to a crumble) and drizzle with more salted caramel sauce.

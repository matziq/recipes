Reuben Sandwich

A grilled rye sandwich layered with corned beef, sauerkraut, Swiss cheese, and a homemade Russian-style dressing.

Yield: 3 servings • Prep: 10 min • Cook: 5 min • Total: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb corned beef
  • ½ tsp olive oil
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 2 Tbsp unsalted butter (room temp)
  • 1/2 cup sauerkraut (drained, storebought or homemade)
  • 6 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 1/2 tsp chili garlic sauce
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 ½ tsp creamy horseradish
  • 1 garlic clove (minced)
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp onion (finely grated)
  • ⅛ tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the mayonnaise, chili garlic sauce, lemon juice, horseradish, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, grated onion, and paprika. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then set the dressing aside.
  2. Warm the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the corned beef and heat it through.
  3. Butter one side of each rye bread slice and place the slices butter-side down.
  4. Spread dressing on the bread, then layer on corned beef, Swiss cheese, and drained sauerkraut. Close the sandwiches.
  5. Cook the sandwiches in a hot skillet over medium heat for 4-6 minutes, turning once, until the cheese melts and the bread is crisp.

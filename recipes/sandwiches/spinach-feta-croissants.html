Spinach Feta Croissants

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Italian salad dressing
  • 6 croissants split
  • 3 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 4 plump tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese

Instructions

  1. Brush salad dressing over the cut sides of croissants. On the bottom halves, layer the spinach, tomatoes and feta cheese; replace tops.

Prep/Total: 20 min

Yield: 6 servings
